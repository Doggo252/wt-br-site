Ground Assault is a co-op PvE mode in which your team uses tanks, and later helicopters and temporary aircraft, to keep waves of AI vehicles out of a capture zone. Because the enemy is predictable and the payouts are steady, it is a relaxed way to farm SL and RP, and a friendly place for newer players to learn their vehicles. For the rules shared with Air Assault, see [Assault Mode](/guide/game-modes/assault-mode/).

## How a match runs

- The zone you defend is the area around your spawn. Inside it you can restock ammunition, repair and replace lost crew.
- After the match starts you get 30 seconds to move into position before the first enemies appear.
- There are 11 waves. A wave is over once every enemy in it is destroyed, followed by a 5-second pause in which your team gets a few tickets back and equipment pickups appear. Driving over a pickup instantly repairs your vehicle and refills its ammunition, and pickups can also restore crew. They can turn up outside the zone.
- Any AI vehicle that gets into the zone makes it flash and starts draining your tickets, faster the more enemies are inside. Enemies inside the zone move more slowly and shoot less accurately. You lose when your tickets hit zero.
- A counter in the bottom-left corner shows how many waves are left, including the current one.
- A match usually lasts 20 to 30 minutes, depending on how quickly the team clears waves.
- Enemies always come from seven fixed directions, picked at random. Their numbers depend on your bracket, on how many players are active, and on how far you have got: the game raises enemy counts up to 4 times per battle, roughly every 3 to 4 waves.
- Every spawn costs Silver Lions: 0.8 times the vehicle's Arcade repair cost. Respawns are unlimited.

## Brackets

Your vehicle's battle rating puts you in one of six brackets. Higher brackets bring more modern enemies that are harder to kill.

| Bracket | Battle rating |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0 – 2.3 |
| 2 | 2.7 – 3.7 |
| 3 | 4.0 – 5.0 |
| 4 | 5.3 – 6.3 |
| 5 | 6.7 – 8.0 |
| 6 | 8.3 and up |

## Wave order

Waves get bigger as the match goes on. Tanks come in four weights: light, medium, intermediate (a tougher medium, sometimes called medium-heavy) and heavy. Some waves also bring hostile air support (CAS), and in brackets 5 and 6, enemy anti-aircraft vehicles (SPAA) join waves 2, 5, 8 and 11.

| Wave | Tanks | Also in the wave |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Light | |
| 2 | Medium | CAS, SPAA |
| 3 | Heavy | |
| 4 | Medium | |
| 5 | Intermediate | SPAA |
| 6 | Heavy | |
| 7 | Light | CAS |
| 8 | Intermediate | SPAA |
| 9 | Heavy | |
| 10 | Intermediate | CAS |
| 11 | Heavy | SPAA |

Enemy SPAA only shoot at aircraft and helicopters. They arrive 20 seconds after the tanks in their wave, the game usually sends about one per player flying a helicopter, and they aim extremely well. They are deadly inside 3 km and can reach you very soon after you lift off the helipad.

## What you can bring

In brackets 1 to 5 your Ground Assault lineup can hold any ground vehicles. In bracket 6 (the top tier) you can add helicopters as well.

Kills also unlock temporary aircraft in every bracket. Ground or air kills both count:

| Kills needed | Brackets 1–5 | Bracket 6 |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Fighter | Fighter |
| 3 | Attacker | Rocket-armed helicopter |
| 8 | Bomber | ATGM-armed helicopter |

Each of these comes with a timer that limits how long you can stay in it: short for fighters, longer for bombers. The aircraft on offer roughly match your bracket, from interwar and early WWII propeller planes in bracket 1 to Cold War and modern jets in brackets 5 and 6.

## Maps and enemies

There are 7 maps: Ardennes, Sinai, Kursk, Mozdok, Fulda, Japan and Maginot Line. You cannot choose which one you get. The enemy's nation matches the map, except on Fulda, whose bracket 6 waves are a mix of NATO vehicles.

| Brackets | What you face | Notable enemies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Interwar and early WWII tanks in bracket 1; later, better-armoured WWII vehicles from bracket 2 | Valentine I, S.35, Churchill III / Pz.Kpfw. Churchill, KV-1 (L-11), ARL-44 (ACL-1) |
| 3 and 4 | A big step up: stronger guns and thicker armour | Tiger H1 / Heavy Tank No.6, Tiger II (P), T26E1-1, Black Prince, M4A3E2 |
| 5 and 6 | Late WWII and Cold War tanks in bracket 5; Cold War to modern tanks in bracket 6; enemy SPAA in both | T32, IS-3, Conqueror, AMX-50 Surbaisse, Leopard 2A6, Type 10 |

In bracket 5, HEAT shells come into their own against the thick armour of late-war and early Cold War tanks, especially the Soviet heavies.

## Rewards

You earn normal Silver Lions and Research Points using the Arcade multiplier, and premium vehicle bonuses, premium account time and vehicle talismans all add to it. Your first Ground Assault battle of the day also gives a free SL or RP booster, usually a bigger one if you win, and a win may add a single backup for ground vehicles.

Boosters you activate yourself do not apply in Ground Assault, so save them for other modes.

## Tips

- **Pick your spot.** Enemies always come from set locations, so sitting to the side of or behind a spawn area gives you quick, safe kills. Stay close to cover and keep out of open ground, where the sheer number of AI guns will take you apart.
- **Bring penetration.** Have at least one vehicle in your lineup that can deal with the heavy tanks. If nothing you own can, flank their spawn areas instead.
- **Stop the leaders.** Knocking out the tracks of the front vehicles halts the column, but the AI repairs damaged parts, including the engine, and puts out fires.
- **Watch the sky.** Shooting down enemy planes is optional but strongly advised, because some carry weapons that can kill you in one pass.
- **Save your big aircraft.** Keep attacker and bomber spawns for a large or heavy wave.
- **Helicopter pilots, respect the SPAA.** Keep your distance and kill them with ATGMs if you can. Note too that some AI tanks, such as the Russian tanks on Kursk, can shoot at helicopters with roof-mounted machine guns, and the AI stays very accurate even at full speed.
- **Play as a team.** Ground Assault is easy to win when everyone pulls their weight on kills.
