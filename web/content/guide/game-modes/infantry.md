Infantry is the newest branch of War Thunder, joining aircraft, ground vehicles and ships. It brings urban combat, building assaults, firefights and sniper duels, with armoured vehicles and aircraft also in the mix. For now it is only playable in a closed beta test (CBT), and it is still under active development, so expect the mode to keep changing, possibly a lot, before release.

## Joining the closed beta

- **Apply first.** You have to send a participation request on the official website in advance.
- **Watch your inbox.** Invitations go out before each test stage, and a single invitation covers every stage. Once you have one, you have access to infantry testing.
- **Where to play.** Beta battles appear in the **Events and Tournaments** section.
- **Limited windows.** Each stage only runs for a set time, so follow the official channels for the dates of upcoming stages.

### Feedback and streaming

Report bugs through the official bug reporting service using the "Infantry" category, and use the dedicated forum section for discussion and suggestions. If you have beta access but the forum section does not show up for you, sign out and sign back in.

Streaming and recording the beta is allowed, but bug reports belong on the Community Bug Reporting platform under "Infantry", not anywhere else. Detailed stats and mechanics for the mode will only be published closer to its full release, since so much is still changing.

## How the tests have evolved

| Stage | Dates | What was tested |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | December 24–26, 2025 | USA and USSR only. Both Arcade and Realistic battles. Each spawn gave you a unit of several soldiers to command or switch between. Small arms plus various drones; enough points let you respawn in an armoured vehicle or helicopter. Map: Granitograd. |
| 2 | March 25–27, 2026 | Still USA and USSR, but Realistic only, in 40-player and 128-player formats. You spawned alone with no AI squad and could spawn next to a player from your own squad. New map Al-Massa, new weapons, soldier classes, and selectable equipment whose weight affected aim and movement speed. |
| 2.1 | April 29 – May 1, 2026 | Granitograd returned alongside Al-Massa. Same core format (Realistic, 40 or 128 players, solo spawns), with newer vehicles and improved controls, sound and optimisation. |
| 3 | July 14–17, 2026 | New map Kivu, four times larger than the earlier ones. New weapons, vehicles that carry passengers, and a separate mission mode with five capture points. Smaller changes included a weapon grip setting, redesigned Arcade markers, reworked grenade throwing and revised class presets. |
| 3.1 | From August 5, 2026 | Kivu, Granitograd and Al-Massa, Arcade only. Arcade infantry movement was changed, you could respawn on allies without being in their squad, maps were refined, visual clutter was cut and you could fire from vehicle cabins. |
| 3.2 | August 19 and 22, 2026 | Damage and balance adjustments. |
| 3.3 | August 26 and 29, 2026 | Damage and balance adjustments. |
| 3.4 | September 2, 2026 | Damage and balance adjustments. |
| 3.5 | October 1–2 and October 3–4, 2026 | Weapons, tanks and helicopters from late World War II to the start of the Cold War. Also changes to how loadout weight is calculated, new animations and bug fixes. |

Because the mode is still in testing, treat everything above as a snapshot of the betas rather than a description of the finished game. Keep an eye on [news](/news/) for future test dates.
