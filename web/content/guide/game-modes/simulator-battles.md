Simulator is the mode for players who want to sit inside the vehicle. You see the fight through the eyes of a crew member, enemies are never marked for you, and aircraft controls are set up with joysticks and other dedicated controllers in mind. Beyond the camera, Sim also uses its own battle formats with their own rules.

## What changes compared with other modes

- **First-person view only.** The main seat is the commander in ground vehicles and the pilot in aircraft.
- **No enemy markers.** You have to find enemies yourself, and even friendly markers fade out with distance.
- **Special formats.** Pilots fight in long Enduring Confrontation missions, while tank battles are built around fixed, balanced vehicle lists.

## How teams are put together

### Aircraft

Air Sim runs either as Enduring Confrontation or in custom lobbies that players create themselves. In a lobby, the nations on each side are whatever the room description lists.

Enduring Confrontation is a long mission that you can join at any point and leave without penalty before it ends. It keeps generating tasks; finishing them pays rewards, pushes the front line and adds to your team's score, moving it closer to overall victory. See [Enduring Confrontation](/guide/game-modes/enduring-confrontation/) for how it works in detail.

### Ground vehicles

Teams are formed from a set list of nations and eligible vehicles. For ground vehicles the list names the exact vehicles; for aircraft it gives a BR range. Several ground Sim battles with different vehicle sets can be on offer at once, and the sets rotate regularly, being updated once a day.

## Air battles

- **No instructor.** Every engine system is in your hands, and nothing corrects your piloting mistakes.
- **Built for real controls.** Handling is adapted to cockpit-style hardware: stick or yoke, throttle and pedals.
- **Custom lobbies.** Anyone can host a session with its own rules.

You fly the aircraft you placed in your crew slots before the battle. Respawns are unlimited, but only aircraft whose battle rating falls inside the room's range can be used.

When creating a room, you choose:

- the battle rating range,
- the nations on each team,
- the mission.

In Enduring Confrontation, teams fight for control of the map by completing missions such as destroying marked ground targets, knocking out bases and wrecking enemy airfields.

## Ground battles

- **Combined arms.** Tanks, planes and helicopters share the battlefield.
- **Real sight positions.** A ground vehicle's sight sits where it was mounted on the real vehicle, not inside the gun barrel.

### Spawning

Both ground vehicles and aircraft (planes and helicopters) take part. How often you can come back depends on your spawn points, and in Ground Sim these are limited and cannot be earned during the battle. You can take a plane or helicopter right from the start; it always appears on the airfield or helipad. Each vehicle can only be spawned a limited number of times, and backups give you extra spawns. You join according to pre-set vehicle lists that are refreshed once a day.

### Aiming

There is no aim assistant and no lead marker. The exceptions are vehicles with radar tracking, such as the German Gepard anti-aircraft vehicle, and aircraft fitted with a ballistic computer.

### Mission types

| Mission | How to win |
|---|---|
| Domination | Hold the three points on the map for longer than the enemy until their team points run out. |
| Conquest | Hold the single central point for longer than the enemy until their team points run out. |
| Battle | Be the first to capture the enemy's point. Capturing takes much longer here than in other missions. |

For the more forgiving modes, see [Arcade Battles](/guide/game-modes/arcade-battles/) and [Realistic Battles](/guide/game-modes/realistic-battles/).
