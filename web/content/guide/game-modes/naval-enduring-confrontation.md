Naval Enduring Confrontation is a long, objective-driven battle for ships. It sits in the **Events and Tournaments** section from Thursday to Monday and alternates between Arcade and Realistic every 24 hours. A match can run for up to three hours, and it revolves around taking ports, attacking or protecting convoys and finishing tasks for points. Respawns are unlimited, but each one costs Silver Lions, and higher-BR ships also cost spawn points.

## How a match is scored

Both teams race to 15,000 points. On screen this appears as tickets counting down for the other side, and you win when the enemy runs out.

- **Silver Lions.** Every respawn costs SL based on the ship's repair cost.
- **Spawn points (SP).** Ships at BR 5.3 and above also need SP, and the price rises with BR. Aircraft cost a fixed amount of SP.
- **Earning SP.** You get SP by dealing damage, scoring kills and helping teammates.
- **Convoys.** While a convoy is active, it gives your team extra places to spawn.

## Lineup rules

To be let in at all, you need a destroyer or light cruiser at BR 4.7 to 5.0, and every ship in the lineup must be BR 3.7 or higher. Beyond that, the wider your choice the better:

- a cruiser at BR 5.3 or 5.7, which costs little SP to spawn;
- battleships (BR 6.0 and up) for when you have banked a lot of SP;
- coastal vessels, which sometimes get spawns closer to convoys;
- as many ships, across as many BRs, as your hangar and crews allow;
- aircraft, if you want to fight in the air as well.

## Spending spawn points well

It is tempting to sit on your SP until you can afford a big ship, such as 600 SP for a BR 7.0 battleship. Experienced players' advice is that it usually pays better to get a BR 5.3 cruiser out for 90 SP as early as you can. Its 152 mm or 203 mm guns and extra toughness outclass the 127 mm guns typical of BR 4.7 to 5.0 destroyers, so it does more damage per hit, survives longer and contributes more to port captures and convoy defence. All of that adds up to SP coming in faster.

## Your first spawn

Do not spawn the moment the match starts. Give the map a short while to update, usually under a minute, check where the convoy has appeared and spawn there for the best early SP.

Open with a BR 5.0 destroyer or light cruiser: it needs no SP and costs little in Silver Lions. In some nations, a boat spawn with torpedo boats or strong coastal ships such as the Russian SKR-7 can work very well, but only if you are comfortable handling them. Otherwise you are just handing the enemy easy kills.

## During the battle

- **Leave convoy cargo ships and tankers alone.** They can give you useful spawn locations, for example close to an enemy port. Spend your shells on the defenders, and clear every threat so the objective, usually an enemy port, can be taken.
- **Keep an eye on the whole map.** If the enemy is hitting a port somewhere else, you may want to spawn there after your next death, or even leave your current ship early to get there.

## Map checks and carriers

Press M to open the map. Objectives, including carriers, are listed on the left. Wait until the enemy carrier shows up in that list before going after it, and coordinate with your team in chat or on voice.

A carrier only counts once a friendly ship or plane has spotted it by coming within 4.5 km. Sink it before it has been spotted and you get nothing for it.

Check the map often: objectives change throughout the battle, and staying near the active ones keeps the SP coming.

## Ports and torpedo nets

Ports are labelled A, B, C and so on, and each is ringed by a torpedo net. Any torpedo that tries to cross the net explodes on it. When attacking a port, you can use that to your advantage by positioning just inside or just outside the net, depending on where the enemy fleet is spawning.

- If a distance to the port is shown, you are outside it and its nets.
- If the port's letter under the score bar is framed by a white box and shows no distance, you are within the port.
- To capture a port, destroy its AI artillery, typically 5 or more guns, along with any enemy ships inside. Only then does the capture start.

Hitting the artillery can be fiddly. Fire single ranging shots, and slow down or come to a full stop if that helps your aim.

## More tips

- **Upgrade as you go.** Start in a BR 5.0 light cruiser or destroyer, then move up to BR 5.3 cruisers or bigger once your SP allows.
- **Practise long-range gunnery.** Engagements are often at long range, so get comfortable with the rangefinder in test drives or random battles.
- **Use aircraft deliberately.** They cost SP, so fly fighters to intercept bombers or bombers to hit objectives. The air side of the mode is covered in [Enduring Confrontation](/guide/game-modes/enduring-confrontation/).
- **Be patient.** Big maps and long matches reward players who stay on the objectives and talk to their team.

## Working with your team

Naval EC is hard to read at first, and teamwork makes the biggest difference:

- Regular EC players tend to be helpful, so ask questions in battle.
- Use team chat to share enemy positions and call for help on port attacks.
- Voice chat, such as the Discord servers many squadrons use, makes a team much more effective.
- If you are an experienced squadron player, posting your Discord invite in chat is a good way to bring new players in.
