Air Assault is the aircraft half of War Thunder's co-op PvE mode. Your team protects a base from waves of AI bombers and attackers, while ground howitzers chip away at it from a distance. Like Ground Assault, it is a low-stress way to farm SL and RP. It asks for less positioning than the ground version, but a team that does not coordinate can lose it surprisingly quickly. For the rules shared by both versions, see [Assault Mode](/guide/game-modes/assault-mode/).

## The objective

The base sits in the middle of the map, below the player spawn. Every successful enemy attack on it lowers your ticket count, shown at the top of the screen. You win by beating every wave and lose if your tickets reach zero.

The howitzers work differently from the aircraft. They never attack the base directly; instead, as long as any of them are alive, they keep draining your tickets.

Respawns are unlimited, and each spawn costs Silver Lions equal to 0.8 times the vehicle's Arcade repair cost.

## Brackets

The battle rating of your aircraft sets the bracket, and with it how modern and how tough the enemy aircraft are.

| Bracket | Battle rating |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0 – 2.3 |
| 2 | 2.7 – 3.7 |
| 3 | 4.0 – 5.0 |
| 4 | 5.3 – 6.3 |
| 5 | 6.7 – 12.7 |

No bracket covers 13.0 and above, so 12.7 is the highest battle rating allowed. Going lower is fine, though: you can take a propeller fighter into the top bracket if you want to.

## Waves

There are 15 waves in total, and the number still to come is shown in the bottom-left corner. A new wave arrives when every enemy plane is down or when the countdown between waves reaches zero. The countdown resets with each wave but starts from a lower value as the mission goes on, so near the end you may be fighting two waves at once.

| Enemy type | Waves |
|---|---|
| Bombers | 1, 4, 7, 10, 15 |
| Attackers | 2, 5, 8, 11, 13 |
| Random | 3, 6, 9, 12, 14 |

"Random" means a 50/50 chance of bombers or attackers.

- **Bombers** appear about 20 km from the base at 3,500 m and fly straight at their target.
- **Attackers** also start about 20 km out but much lower, at 1,500 m. Just before reaching the base they dive to release their bombs, and you can still catch them during that dive.

How many enemies spawn depends on the bracket, on how many players are active and on how many waves you have already beaten. With only five players, for example, the game sends half the usual number of planes. As waves fall, the game increases enemy numbers up to 4 times per battle, roughly every 3 to 4 waves.

### Artillery

Howitzer waves follow no fixed schedule. They can first show up from wave 2, and the chance rises with each wave that passes. Once artillery has appeared, it will not come again until wave 8, when the chance resets and it can spawn once more.

## Maps and enemies

There are 7 maps, and the map you get is random. The enemy aircraft belong to the nation that fits the map, and they become more modern, and harder to bring down, the higher the bracket.

## Rewards

Silver Lions and Research Points are paid at the normal rate with the Arcade multiplier, and premium vehicle bonuses, premium account time and vehicle talismans all raise it. Scoring 1,500 points in a battle is enough for the full reward.

Your first Air Assault battle of the day also hands out a free booster. Whether it boosts SL or RP is random, and its size depends on the result: a win can bring a booster of up to 300% plus a universal backup.

Boosters you activate yourself do not work in Air Assault.

## How to win

- **Never ignore the howitzers.** They can drain your tickets very fast and end the match early. Keep at least 3 players on artillery duty, but do not let the whole team chase them while the bombers get through.
- **Attack from above or below.** Bombers and attackers with turrets are dangerous from behind. Dive in, fire a burst and break away quickly. Head-on passes are also worth taking, because these AI planes do not use their forward-firing guns.
- **Reload between waves.** Once a wave is cleared, press R to reload your guns so you start the next one with full ammunition.
- **Use air-to-ground missiles at the top.** In the highest bracket, missiles such as the AGM-65 Maverick and Kh-29 work extremely well against every enemy type thanks to their large explosive mass.
- **Coordinate.** With everyone doing their share of the killing, Air Assault is very hard to lose.
