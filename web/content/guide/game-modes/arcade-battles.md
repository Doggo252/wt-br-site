Arcade is the entry point to War Thunder. Vehicles are easier to drive and fly, their mobility and weapon handling are boosted to keep the pace high, and every vehicle type gets some form of aiming help. Teams are not built by nation, so any mix of vehicles inside the battle rating spread can land on either side, and you can easily meet the exact vehicle you are using.

## What every Arcade battle has in common

- **Boosted handling.** Speed and agility are tuned up to fit the quicker rhythm of the mode.
- **Several lives.** Losing a vehicle does not end your match: you pick another one from your lineup and go again.
- **Shared spotting.** Once you or a teammate can see an enemy, it gets a marker in the world and an icon on the minimap.

## Air battles

### Help you get in the air

- You cannot stall into a spin, and no manoeuvre is hard enough to tear the airframe apart. This applies to planes and helicopters alike.
- When your guns and ordnance run dry, they refill in flight. There is no need to land.
- A lead marker shows roughly where to aim so your rounds meet an enemy aircraft, assuming it keeps flying the way it is going.

### Respawns

Your lineup is the only real limit. Each aircraft can come back once more with a backup, so a full set of 10 crew slots with a backup for every plane works out to as many as 20 spawns in one battle.

### Mission types

A team loses when its tickets hit zero or when none of its players can spawn.

| Mission | What you do |
|---|---|
| Ground Strike | AI ground units and bombing points are spread over the map. Killing ground units or aircraft and bombing enemy points or the airfield all cost the enemy tickets. |
| Front Line | Same targets as Ground Strike, but the ground units are bunched along a front running through the middle of the map. |
| Domination | Land on contested airfields to take them; the other team then bleeds tickets slowly. AI ground columns on both sides will also start capturing if nobody stops them. |
| Air Domination | Hold a central zone by keeping more aircraft inside it than the enemy. |
| Air Conquest | A mix of the two above: two airfields to capture plus a central zone. |

## Ground battles

Tanks move and turn faster than they would in real life. Spotted enemies show on the minimap and carry a tag with the player's name, the vehicle and the distance to it.

### Aiming and damage aids

- **Aim assistant.** A crosshair shows where your shell will land at the current gun elevation and is coloured by the chance to penetrate at that spot: red means unlikely, yellow means possible, green means likely. Shell dispersion can still put the round somewhere else. The colour only compares the effective (angled) thickness with your shell's theoretical penetration at that range; it does not account for ricochets.
- **Lead marker for aircraft.** Your gunner works out roughly where to shoot at an enemy plane and marks the spot.
- **Modules never fully break.** A damaged engine or gun keeps working at reduced strength. A hit barrel is less accurate and fires shells with less energy, so they penetrate less. Even a badly damaged engine will still get you to a capture point so you can repair there if you cannot do it yourself.

### Spawns and aircraft

You can bring up to three ground vehicles into one battle. Kills, assists and captures earn airstrike points, which you can spend to take over a random aircraft from one of three pools:

| Aircraft | Cost |
|---|---|
| Fighter | 1 point |
| Strike aircraft | 2 points |
| Bomber | 3 points |

Each aircraft is yours for one minute and flies by Air Arcade rules. In battles at BR 6.0 and above, a bomber carrying a nuclear bomb costs 15 airstrike points, and the team that manages to drop it wins on the spot.

When an attacker or bomber has released all of its suspended weapons, or a fighter has no enemy aircraft left to fight, an evacuation zone appears. Fly to its icon at any altitude and you leave the aircraft automatically, earning the "Safe and Sound" award and a random bonus of Silver Lions, air battle points or artillery strike points.

### Mission types

Destroying enemy ground vehicles drains their tickets. A team loses at zero tickets or when nobody on it can spawn a ground vehicle.

- **Domination:** three capture points. The side holding more of them makes the other bleed tickets, and the bleed scales with the gap, so 3–0 drains faster than 2–1.
- **Conquest:** a single capture point; whoever holds it drains the enemy.
- **Battle:** two points, one owned by each team at the start. Capturing is much slower than in the other modes, and a team that loses its own point starts shedding tickets very fast and usually loses soon after.

## Naval battles

Every ship gets a large boost to engine power and turning speed. Spotted ships appear on the minimap and carry nametags, as in Ground Arcade.

### Assists

- Shells, depth charges, torpedoes and missiles refill on their own, anywhere on the map, with no need to visit a reload point. Secondary weapons also deploy instantly.
- Gunners range the target themselves and refine the range after each near miss. Your guns simply aim where your cursor points.
- A marker suggests where to lead a torpedo launch, and large-calibre shells leave a visible tracer.
- Horizontal turret traverse is twice as fast. Torpedoes also get a speed multiplier that depends on how far they have run: 3 up to 4 km, dropping linearly from 3 to 1 between 4 and 8 km, and 1 beyond 8 km.

### Spawns

You can spawn a ship up to 3 times, and reserve vessels can come back up to 3 times for free without using backups.

Spawn points earned in battle also let you take to the air, at most twice per battle:

- **Your own aircraft**, as long as it is no more than one BR step above your highest-rated ship. A BR 3.3 boat, for example, allows aircraft up to BR 3.7.
- **A random aircraft** of the same nation, rated up to 1.0 below your top ship, from one of two pools: fighters, or bombers and strike aircraft.

Picking the same category a second time doubles its spawn cost.

### Mission types

Sinking enemy vessels drains their tickets. A team loses at zero or when nobody can spawn a ship or aircraft.

- **Domination:** three points, with ticket bleed proportional to how many more points one side holds.
- **Conquest:** one large capture area. The team with more ships inside drains the other, faster the bigger the difference in ship count.
- **Battle:** two points. Taking both reveals a third, and capturing that one wins the match.
- **Encounter:** each team is joined by an AI convoy of cargo ships, destroyers and light cruisers, and sinking those also costs the enemy tickets.
