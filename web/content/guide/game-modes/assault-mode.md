Assault is War Thunder's cooperative mode: instead of fighting other players, a team of up to 10 holds off waves of AI enemies. It comes in two flavours. In Air Assault you defend a base from bombers, attackers and artillery; in Ground Assault you stop columns of AI tanks from taking a central zone. Both run on Arcade rules for damage, flight models and other mechanics, including rearming in the air.

## Rules both versions share

- **Unlimited respawns.** You can spawn the same vehicle as often as you like, so a lineup of one vehicle is enough.
- **You pay to spawn, not to repair.** Each spawn costs Silver Lions equal to 0.8 times the vehicle's Arcade repair cost. Being destroyed does not cost anything extra.
- **Rewards follow survival.** Your payout depends on how long you last in the mission. Scoring 1,500 points in a battle is enough for the full RP and SL reward, and everyone past that mark receives the same amount.
- **Daily bonus.** Your first Assault battle of the day gives an extra reward that grows with the number of waves your team beats. The best possible result is a 300% SL or RP booster plus a universal backup for any vehicle.

## Levels by battle rating

The vehicle you bring decides which level you play, and the level decides how tough the AI is. Higher levels spawn enemies that are harder to destroy.

| Level | Air Assault | Ground Assault |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0 – 2.3 | 1.0 – 2.3 |
| 2 | 2.7 – 3.7 | 2.7 – 3.7 |
| 3 | 4.0 – 5.0 | 4.0 – 5.0 |
| 4 | 5.3 – 6.3 | 5.3 – 6.3 |
| 5 | 6.7 – 12.7 | 6.7 – 8.0 |
| 6 | none | 8.3 and up (tanks and helicopters) |

You are allowed to bring something rated below a level's range. Propeller fighters can, for instance, take part in the highest Air Assault level.

## Air Assault at a glance

The base sits at the centre of the map, below the player spawn. The AI attacks with bombers, attackers and ground artillery over 15 waves, and the next wave arrives either when every enemy plane is down or when the countdown between waves runs out. That countdown starts lower as the mission goes on, so late in a match two waves can be in the air at once. Artillery cannot hit the base directly but steadily wears down its health while it survives. Fewer players means fewer enemies: with only five players, the game sends half the usual number of planes.

Full details, wave order and tactics: [Air Assault](/guide/game-modes/air-assault/).

## Ground Assault at a glance

Here you hold a zone in the middle of the map against incoming tanks. Any AI vehicle that reaches the zone makes it flash and starts draining your tickets, faster the more of them get in, and the match is lost at zero. Enemies slow down and shoot less accurately once inside the zone, and beating a wave gives back a few tickets. Helicopters are only allowed in the top bracket (BR 8.3 and up).

Full details, wave order, maps and tactics: [Ground Assault](/guide/game-modes/ground-assault/).
