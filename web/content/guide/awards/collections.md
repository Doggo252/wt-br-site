Collections are themed sets of vehicle cosmetics, such as decals and decorations, that you gather in the hangar under **Player Profile → Collections**. Finishing a set earns a special reward that cannot be obtained any other way, and that reward does not have to match the set: completing a collection of decals might, for example, give you a decoration.

## Collections and super collections

Both live in the same Player Profile → Collections menu. A super collection is built from the rewards of several regular collections, and completing one gives an even rarer reward.

## Using the Collections window

- Items you already have on your account are highlighted.
- Every item's description, both in the game and on the Market, names the collection it belongs to.
- Clicking an item shows what you need to do to get it, lets you preview it on a vehicle, lets you activate its coupon if you have one, and can take you to its Market page.

## Where items come from

- event tasks;
- permanent in-game tasks;
- buying them with Golden Eagles;
- buying them on the [Market](/market/).

Old or rare items that belong to a current collection are not necessarily gone for good. They can come back as event rewards or be offered for sale in some other way.

## Collecting without the Market

If you play on PlayStation or Xbox, or cannot use the Market for any other reason, there are two trophies to fill the gap: one for decals and one for decorations. Each contains one eligible item from every available collection of that type, so the decorations trophy can give you an item from any decoration collection. It only gives you items that you do not already have on your account.

Activate any item coupons you own before opening one of these trophies. If the trophy gives you an item that you are holding as a coupon, that coupon can no longer be activated.
