Match awards are small achievements that you earn during a battle and that reset when it ends, so you can win them again next match. Each one pays out Silver Lions, some count towards daily missions and wagers, and some reward keeping up a run of good play within a single battle.

In Realistic and Simulator battles most awards use a x2 or x3 multiplier, so a threshold of 12 enemies can be reached with only 6 or 4 actual kills, depending on the mode. Awards marked "no multiplier" below always need the real count.

## Top-of-the-match awards

These go to the single best player in a category. Most come in two versions: a big one if you clear the threshold, and a smaller one if you top the category below it. For Wingman and most of the smaller versions, a tie goes to the player with the higher score.

| Category | Big award | Smaller award |
|---|---|---|
| Aircraft destroyed in Air Battles | Hero of the Sky: at least 12 | Terror of the Sky: 11 or fewer |
| Player ground vehicles destroyed | Heavy Metal Hero: at least 10 in Arcade and Realistic, 7 in Simulator | Heavy Metal Fury: up to 9 in Arcade and Realistic, 6 in Simulator |
| AI ground targets destroyed (ships and other naval targets excluded) | Thunderer: at least 18, and more than any other player on either team | Antimech: 17 or fewer |
| AI naval units destroyed | Tsunami: at least 18 | Rogue Wave: 17 or fewer |
| Player ships destroyed in Naval Battles | Terror of the Sea: at least 9 in Arcade and Realistic | Terror of the Ocean: up to 8 in Arcade and Realistic |
| Assists on enemy kills | Wingman: at least 8 | On Hand: 7 or fewer, no multiplier, and only kill assists count (not scouting assists) |
| Kills of vehicles one rank above yours | Punisher: at least 5, no multiplier | Balancer: 4 or fewer, no multiplier |
| Kills without losing a vehicle | Survivor: no vehicle lost (aircraft, tank or ship), more kills than anyone on either team, at least 5, no multiplier | Bulletproof: no vehicle lost, more kills than everyone else who also lost nothing, 4 or fewer, no multiplier |

## Battle milestones

- **First strike!** and **Final blow!** go to whoever delivers the opening and the closing blow of the battle. Landing both earns **Mission Maker**.
- **The last man standing!** for being the last player left in the mission.
- **The Best Squad** goes to the squad that racked up more squad streaks than any other during the match.

## Nuclear awards

- **Doomsday!** for making a nuclear strike and winning the mission with it.
- **One minute to midnight** for finishing a mission in a nuclear bomber without dropping the bomb.
- **The end is near** for shooting down a nuclear carrier after it had already dropped.
- **Peaceful atom** for shooting down a nuclear carrier before it could drop.

## Kill counts and special kills

| Award | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fighter Hunter / Fighters' Nightmare | At least 5 / at least 10 fighters destroyed |
| Bomber Hunter / Bombers' Nightmare | At least 5 / at least 10 bombers or assault planes destroyed |
| Tanks Hunter / Tanks' Nightmare | At least 5 / at least 10 tanks or tank destroyers destroyed |
| Wing Breaker | Most air kills in a tank battle, with at least 5 aircraft shot down from ground vehicles |
| Fire Arrows | Destroy an enemy plane with rockets |
| Surprise! | Destroy an enemy plane with bombs |
| Battered! | Knock out at least 30% of the crew survivability of a light or heavy cruiser, battleship or battlecruiser |
| Sniper | Destroy an enemy vehicle from long range |
| Rank does not matter | Destroy a vehicle of the same type rated 1.0 to 2.0 BR above yours |
| Skill matters | Destroy a vehicle of the same type rated 2.3 to 5.0 BR above yours |
| God Mode! | Destroy a vehicle of the same type rated more than 5.0 BR above yours |
| Professional: xN | Destroy 5 vehicles in one life; each further kill in that life adds another, so 7 kills earns 3 |
| Soft Landing | Land an aircraft that is critically damaged |

Two awards are for ground vehicles only:

- **Without a miss:** destroy three or more enemies without a single one of your shells missing. One-shot kills are not required. Three kills in a row earns one award, four earns two, and so on. All of your shots count, as do kills made with artillery, but artillery misses do not.
- **Adamant:** survive more than 9 non-critical hits in your ground vehicle without being destroyed.

## Streaks

| Award | How it works |
|---|---|
| Multi strike! | Destroy several player-controlled vehicles in quick succession. From the first kill to the last may take no more than 10 seconds in Arcade, or 30 seconds in Realistic and Simulator. Stages: Double strike!, Triple strike!, Multi Strike xN! |
| Ground Multi strike | In an aircraft, destroy AI ground vehicles in succession within 5 seconds from first to last. Stages: Triple strike!, Multi Strike xN! |
| Water Multi strike! | In an aircraft, destroy AI ships in succession within 5 seconds from first to last. Stages: Triple strike!, Multi Strike xN! |
| Assist streak: xX! | Help destroy enemy vehicles X times in a row |
| Shadow strike streak: xX! | Destroy enemy vehicles X times in a row without being hit yourself |
| Base capturer streak: xX! | Capture a base X times in a row |

## Teamwork awards

### Rescues

Each rescue award is for killing an enemy player shortly after they critically damaged a friendly unit.

| Award | Who you saved | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fighter Rescuer | A friendly fighter | |
| Bomber Rescuer | A friendly bomber | |
| Ground Force Rescuer | A friendly ground unit | Earned while flying |
| Water Troops Rescuer | A friendly ship | Earned while flying |
| Tank Rescuer | A friendly ground unit | |
| Ship Rescuer | A friendly ship | |

### Helping your team

- **Intelligence:** a teammate destroys an enemy you marked. **According to Intelligence** is the reverse: you destroy an enemy a teammate marked.
- **Avenger:** destroy the enemy who just killed a teammate. **Eye for Eye:** destroy the enemy who killed you.
- **Firefighter:** put out a fire on an allied vehicle. **Help with Repairing:** help a teammate repair their vehicle.
- **Base defender:** stop an enemy capturing a base by destroying one of their units.
- **Supporting Fire:** assist a squadmate in destroying an enemy.
- **Teamwork:** given to every squad member near a squadmate who destroys an enemy. "Near" means within 1,000 m for two aircraft, 3,000 m for an aircraft and a tank, and 100 m for two tanks.

## Safe and Sound (Ground Arcade)

When you fly an aircraft during a Ground Arcade battle and keep it alive to the end of its time in the air, you earn **Safe and Sound** in its Bomber, Attacker, Fighter or Helicopter version. See [Arcade Battles](/guide/game-modes/arcade-battles/) for how the evacuation zone works.

The Superhero profile title asks you to complete the Hero of the Sky, Survivor, Wingman, Thunderer and Punisher challenges; see [Titles](/guide/awards/titles/).
