Decorations are 3D cosmetic items that you attach to a vehicle to make it look like yours. They work on ground vehicles and ships but not on aircraft, and they are purely visual: a helmet or a shield strapped to the hull gives no extra armour. Camouflage foliage can break up part of a vehicle's outline, but far less than a proper camouflage pattern, and decorations lose their detail with distance. Past close range, a decorated vehicle looks just like a bare one.

## Ground vehicle categories

| Category | What is in it |
|---|---|
| Thunder League | Unique items for owners of the Golden Thunder League dog tag |
| Skulls | Animal skulls of various kinds |
| Road signs | An assortment of road signs |
| Roundels & insignia | National roundels and unit insignia |
| Machine guns | Static machine guns from different eras, from the British Bren and Soviet Maxim to the modern M240 |
| Rifles | Semi-automatic and sniper rifles |
| Submachine guns | WWII-era SMGs such as the Sten, Thompson, PPSh-41 and MP-40 |
| Assault rifles | Assault rifles from several countries, some with underslung grenade launchers |
| Weapons | Other weapons, such as the PIAT or a biathlon rifle |
| Helmets | Infantry and tank crew helmets |
| Ammunition | Vests, body armour and related gear from WWI to the present |
| Shields | Historical shields |
| Hats | Novelty headwear: clown hats, pirate hats, Viking helmets and more |
| Camouflage foliage | Deciduous, coniferous and palm branches that imitate field camouflage |
| Other | Anything that does not fit elsewhere |

## Ship categories

Ships have two categories: **Roundels and insignia** from various countries, and **Other**, which covers things like tanks, jet skis and ship parts. Some decorations only fit bluewater ships and others only coastal vessels.

## Animated decorations

A few decorations move or add visual effects. They make a vehicle stand out, which is exactly the problem when you are trying to stay hidden: they can give your position away.

## How to get decorations

- **Golden Eagles:** most decorations can be bought this way, apart from some Thunder League exclusives.
- **War Bonds:** some are sold in the War Bond shop.
- **Tasks and achievements:** some are unlocked by completing particular tasks; the decoration's tooltip in the menu tells you which.
- **Events and Battle Pass:** both regularly hand out unique decorations.
- **Market:** decorations released during promotions can be bought from other players on the [Market](/market/).
- **Collections:** completing a decoration collection can earn a bonus item, for example a unique M1 "Born to Kill" helmet for finishing a set of infantry helmets. See [Collections](/guide/awards/collections/).

## Placing decorations

Open a vehicle's page, go to customisation, and buy and place decorations from the customisation menu.

- Each vehicle has up to six decoration slots.
- A decoration you have bought can be used on every ground vehicle you own, as many times as you like, though some cannot be placed more than once on the same vehicle.
- Decorations only attach to parts that cannot be shot or blown off. That rules out gun barrels, machine guns, tracks and detachable parts such as the fenders over the tracks.
- They cannot go on the vehicle's interior bodywork.

### Mind your view

In Realistic and Simulator battles, a decoration can block what the driver or gunner sees. Foliage is the usual culprit when it ends up over vision ports or optics. Put it on armour that does not get in the way, and check the sight lines in X-ray mode before taking the vehicle into battle.
