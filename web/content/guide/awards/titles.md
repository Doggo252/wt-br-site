Titles are short tags you unlock by meeting specific goals. Unlike achievements, they are meant to be shown off: pick one on your player card and it appears under your name in battle for everyone to see. Once you have unlocked a title it stays yours, even if its requirements are changed later.

The titles below fall into two groups. Achievement titles can be earned at any time by playing. Everything else is tied to a specific event, tournament, purchase, Battle Pass season or role.

## Limited titles

### Events

Special events have handed out titles of their own since 2014, for completing event tasks, for taking part, or for buying event bundles. The earliest was Santa's Little Helper from the Winter Holiday Specials of 2014, for destroying 10 ground units with bombs while flying a bomber. Others mark milestones: when the game officially released in December 2016, players received veteran titles based on when they joined, from The Old Guard (2013 or earlier) through Faithful Warrior (2014) and Combat Proven (2015) to Brother in Arms (2016). Some recognise participation, such as World Warrior for taking part in the World War Mode closed beta, or Five Minutes of Fame for winning a spot in the Glorious5 event series.

### Tournaments

A large set of titles rewards tournament play, usually for placing well or for taking part in a specific competition. Some are recurring: Armor of Victory and Wings of Victory come from the Ground Victory and Air Victory tournaments held every May, and Normandy Ace from the D-Day tournaments held every June. 1v1 duel tournaments award 1v1 Duelist, with 1v1 Master-duelist for higher placements, and single-nation 1v1 tank tournaments award American, British, French, German, Japanese or Soviet Tank Expert.

### Store bundles, sales and pre-orders

Many titles come packaged with bundles from the store: stream packs, holiday and seasonal sale bundles, Black Friday packs and pre-order packs. Ground Forces closed beta participants, for example, received First on the Track.

## Other ways to decorate your profile

Titles are not the only profile cosmetic.

- **Profile icons** are the pictures on your player profile. Most can be earned by taking part in events (for example by completing their tasks), by finishing in-game tasks (hover over an icon in the game to see its conditions), or bought with Golden Eagles, on the [Market](/market/) or in the Warbond shop.
- **Profile icon frames and profile backgrounds** were added in Update 2.43.0.37 as further ways to customise your profile, with frames designed to complement your icon.
