Boosters are items you switch on before a battle to add a fixed bonus to the Research Points or Silver Lions you earn. Hovering over a booster in your inventory shows everything about it: what it boosts, by how much, for how many battles and how long it stays usable.

## How the bonus is worked out

A booster's percentage is added to the vehicle's base earnings modifier rather than applied on top of your final income.

Take the BTR-152D, whose base Research Point modifier is 1.42, or 142%. With a 500% RP booster active:

1.42 x (100% + 500%) = 852%

A Premium Account raises a vehicle's earnings by 50% for Silver Lions and 100% for Research Points, also measured against the base modifier. Premium and booster percentages are therefore added together, not multiplied. For a vehicle with a 1.42 modifier, a 500% booster and a 50% premium bonus:

1.42 x (100% + 500% + 50%) = 923%

Our [economy](/economy/) page lists each vehicle's Silver Lion reward multiplier.

## Types of booster

### Who it covers

Most boosters only affect the player who activates them. Boosters with "(all allies)" in their name raise earnings for your whole team, a nice gift to everyone on your side.

### How big

| Type | Bonus range |
|---|---|
| Personal | 10% to 800% |
| Team ("all allies") | 10% or 22% |

The picture on the booster and its description tell you the exact bonus and whether it applies to RP or SL.

### How long

- **Battles.** A booster lasts for one or more battles, listed in its description as a number of "missions". In battles longer than 30 minutes, each 30 minutes counts as a separate battle. In a battle lasting 1 hour 20 minutes, a 1-battle booster only covers the first 30 minutes, while a 10-battle booster covers the whole match and loses 3 battles from its count.
- **Activation time.** Once switched on, a booster stays active only for the time given under "activation time" in its description. When that runs out, it switches off and disappears from your inventory.
- **Activation deadline.** Some boosters also have to be switched on before a deadline, shown under "Valid for", or they vanish unused. Boosters marked "Premium Booster" have no such deadline. Boosters are usually valid for 3 days.

## Where boosters come from

- reaching certain levels of the Battle Pass;
- daily login trophies: the regular daily trophy can contain 1 booster, and the large trophy that comes every 7 days can contain up to 5;
- buying them with Golden Eagles in the in-game shop;
- the War Bonds shop, either as boosters or as Combat Trophies containing them;
- Combat Trophies earned by completing Special Tasks bought in the War Bonds shop;
- the World War Trophy for your first battle of the day;
- prize places in tournaments;
- various in-game events.

## Running several at once

Activating more than one booster of the same type (same coverage, same currency) at the same time is wasteful: every extra one loses much of its effect.

| Boosters of one type active | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 1 | None |
| 2 | 40% |
| 3 | 60% |
| 4 | 80% |
| 5 or more | 90% |

The penalty is handed out by size, not by the order you activated them in: the biggest booster runs at full strength and the smaller ones take the cut. Activate a 10% booster and then a 15% one, and the 10% booster is the one penalised. If the 15% booster runs out while the 10% one is still going, the penalty on the 10% booster is lifted.

You get more out of them by using boosters of the same type one at a time.
