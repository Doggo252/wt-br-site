World War is a squadron-focused mode that War Thunder runs in separate seasons rather than all the time. Each season has its own set of scenarios: historical operations that differ in year, theatre of war, the vehicles taking part and the objectives. Three seasons are covered here, played between May 2019 and July 2020, each with its own vehicle rewards.

## How a season works

**Game days.** A season isn't open around the clock. It is split into game days, each with two play windows: usually 14:00–22:00 GMT, then 01:00–07:00 GMT the following night. Each game day is given over to one operation, and the operations rotate through the season.

**Squadrons and operations.** Operations are created by squadrons, and squadrons are ranked on their own leaderboard. Seasons 2 and 3 also had a separate leaderboard for commanders.

**Individual results.** Players are ranked on a scoreboard for each game day and for the season as a whole. In the second and third seasons, players also collected stars in World War battles; reaching a given number of stars unlocked the next reward on a fixed track.

**When rewards arrive (Season 1).**

| Reward | When it's given |
|---|---|
| Daily tasks | Straight away, once the conditions are met; progress is shown on the "Daily awards" tab of the World War menu |
| Game day results | Within 24 hours for odd-numbered days, within 48 hours for even-numbered days |
| Season results | Within 48 hours of the last game day |

In Seasons 2 and 3, squadron prizes were handed out within 3 days of the season ending.

## Season 1: Recon under Fire

The first season ran over 8 game days between 14 May and 9 June 2019, with results and awards on 10 June. Each of the four operations came up twice. The first day opened earlier than the rest, at 11:00 GMT.

**Daily rewards.** Your first battle of the day earned a World War Trophy containing one of: a +10–30% SL/RP booster, a +10% SL/RP booster for all allies, a random wager, a random "Order" trophy, a random modification for the vehicle you used, or a talisman for that vehicle. A second daily trophy had a richer pool, adding boosters of up to +500% and a set of three universal backups.

**Game day results** (per nation):

| Place on the day's scoreboard | Reward |
|---|---|
| Top 50 | World War Trophy (vehicle) |
| 51–150 | World War Trophy (decoration) |
| 151–400 | Coupon for the decal of the scenario played |

**Season results for all players:**

| Place on the season leaderboard | Reward |
|---|---|
| Top 500 | AMX 50 Surblindé coupon |
| 501–2,000 | Spitfire F. Mk IXc coupon |
| 2,001–4,000 | World War Trophy (vehicle) |
| 4,001–7,500 | World War Trophy (decoration) |

**Squadron results.** You needed at least 8 battles with your squadron to qualify. What you got depended on both your squadron's place and your own place within it:

| Squadron's place | Your place in the squadron | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| 1–25 | 1–20 | AMX 50 Surblindé coupon |
| 1–25 | 21–40 | Spitfire F. Mk IXc coupon |
| 1–25 | 41–80 | World War Trophy (vehicle) |
| 1–25 | 81–128 | World War Trophy (decoration) |
| 26–50 | 1–40 | World War Trophy (vehicle) |
| 26–50 | 41–128 | World War Trophy (decoration) |
| 51–70 | 1–128 | World War Trophy (decoration) |

## Season 2: Attack from the Sea

The second season had 8 game days from 28 January 2020, again with each operation played twice. The last day ran into 12 February, when the season closed and rewards were handed out. The first day started at 12:00 GMT.

**Star track:**

| Stars | Reward |
|---|---|
| 2 | One of the operation decals |
| 3 | BA-11 (USSR) |
| 4 | Camouflage for the BA-11 |
| 5 | One of the operation decals |
| 6 | USS Phelps DD-360 (USA) |
| 7 | Camouflage for the USS Phelps |
| 8 | "The Mighty P" decal (the Phelps emblem) and the title "Veteran of the season: Attack from the sea" |

The reward list also included smaller prizes from the same pool as Season 1's daily trophy: 10–30% SL/RP boosters, a 10% SL/RP booster for all allies, a random wager, a random order, and a modification or talisman for the vehicle you used.

**Squadron and commander prizes:**

| Leaderboard | Places | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Squadrons | 1–3 | USS Phelps DD-360 coupon |
| Squadrons | 4–5 | BA-11 coupon |
| Squadrons | Top 10 | World War season decals, usable until the next World War season starts |
| Squadrons | Top 50 | Squadron medal "Attack from the Sea" |
| Commanders | Top 10 | USS Phelps DD-360 coupon |
| Commanders | 11–50 | BA-11 coupon |

## Season 3: Road to the West

The third season ran from 1 to 20 July 2020 and alternated between two operations. Its first three game days were postponed and played later in the season, so play actually began on 7 July. The postponed days were slotted in between the later ones, giving 10 game days in all, with the last on 19–20 July.

**Star track:**

| Stars | Reward |
|---|---|
| 2 | One of the operation decals |
| 3 | One of the "firearms" 3D decorations |
| 4 | IL-2 (M-82) |
| 5 | Camouflage for the IL-2 (M-82) |
| 6 | One of the operation decals |
| 7 | One of the "firearms" 3D decorations |
| 8 | M60 AMBT |
| 9 | Camouflage for the M60 AMBT |
| 10 | The decal "Emblem of the 600th Armored Brigade of the Israel Defense Forces" and the title "Veteran of the season Road to the West" |

The same pool of smaller prizes as in Season 2 was available alongside the star track.

**Squadron and commander prizes:**

| Leaderboard | Places | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Squadrons | 1–3 | M60 AMBT coupon |
| Squadrons | 4–5 | IL-2 (M-82) coupon |
| Squadrons | Top 10 | World War season decals, usable until the next World War season starts |
| Squadrons | Top 50 | Squadron medal "Road to the West" |
| Commanders | Top 10 | M60 AMBT coupon |
| Commanders | 11–50 | IL-2 (M-82) coupon |
