Aircraft, tanks and ships each have ways of setting the enemy alight. This page covers the two weapons built specifically for it: incendiary bombs and flamethrowers. Both do their real damage to targets that aren't fully enclosed by armour.

## Incendiary bombs

- **Best target:** open-topped vehicles. The burning mixture knocks out exposed crew, and its heat can set the engine on fire.
- **Concealment:** the fire blocks Night Vision Devices and can serve as a rough smoke screen, hiding tanks from enemy eyes for a while.
- **Bases and airfields:** you can use them like ordinary bombs, but kilogram for kilogram they do less damage than general-purpose bombs.

Some players treat them as more show than substance, but in the right spot they do the job.

## Flamethrowers

Flamethrowers arrived in the "Fire and Ice" update, on vehicles that were rewards in Battle Pass Season IX, "Smell of Victory". They have short range and are aimed at open-topped vehicles.

| Vehicle | Type | How the flamethrower is fitted |
|---|---|---|
| TO-55 | Ground | Fuel is carried on board, and the flamethrower is mounted coaxially in the turret |
| Churchill Crocodile | Ground | Fuel rides in a trailer towed behind the tank, and the flame projector sits in the bow gun position |
| LCM(6) Zippo | Naval | Two flamethrowers, port and starboard, behind the 40 mm gun turret. The fuel tanks are in the hull, and each projector shares a small turret with a 7.62 mm M73 machine gun. |

### Fuel

What the flamethrower sprays is liquid fuel set alight, normally a napalm-based mix of the kind found in incendiary bombs like the BLU-1. The Churchill Crocodile's fuel is known as Fuel K, or FTF, Heavy No.1. There's currently no choice of fuel for any flamethrower in the game.

### How the damage works

- **No penetration.** Flame has no armour penetration value. It won't harm the internal modules of a vehicle that is fully armoured or CBRN-sealed.
- **Gaps are fatal.** Against open-topped vehicles, or lightly armoured ones with openings, it does enormous damage.
- **Damage over Time.** No other weapon in the game does this. The ground, objects and targets you hit keep burning for a short while, so damage continues after you stop firing.

In short, save the flamethrower for open-topped and lightly built targets, and don't expect it to do anything to a closed-up tank.
