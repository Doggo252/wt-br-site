Active protection systems (APS) came to ground vehicles with the "Wind of Change" update. They defend the vehicle automatically against ATGMs and slower projectiles such as HEAT-FS shells. An APS takes over a job that is hard to do by hand. Machine-gunning an incoming missile out of the air is possible, but a fast missile, or one you can barely see, rarely gives you the chance. For how missiles are guided, see [Anti-Tank Guided Missiles](/guide/mechanics/atgm/).

## Soft kill and hard kill

APS come in two kinds:

- **Soft-kill** systems leave the projectile intact but confuse its guidance so it misses.
- **Hard-kill** systems spot the incoming projectile and fire a defensive charge to destroy it.

## Electro-optical systems (soft kill)

These are also called infrared countermeasures (IRCM). They use light emitters to throw off a missile's guidance. They only have a narrow set of targets:

- **Affected:** SACLOS missiles that have no electronic counter-countermeasures (ECCM).
- **Not affected:** MCLOS, laser-guided and TV-guided missiles, and SACLOS missiles that do have ECCM.

Soft-kill systems in the game:

|---|---|
| AN/VLQ-8A | USA |
| MUSS | Germany |
| Shtora-1 | USSR |
| JD-3 | China |

### Drozd

Drozd never needs switching on and copes with many kinds of threat. A Doppler radar picks up anything incoming, launchers mounted on both sides of the turret fire the defensive charges, and an electronics box at the turret rear runs the system. It covers the arc in front of it and engages a threat once it is within 7 m.

Its weak point is that it is tied to the turret. It can only protect the direction the turret faces, it needs a clear line of sight to the incoming threat, and at least the APS itself has to be exposed.

You can rely on it while moving, as long as the turret points at the threat, but there are two catches:

- Moving, especially sideways, can let a detected missile slip out of the zone where Drozd engages it.
- If you are driving towards the enemy who fired, Drozd may still destroy the missile, but the wreckage carries enough momentum to damage your tank anyway.
