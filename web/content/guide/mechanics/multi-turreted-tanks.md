The T-35 arrived in Update 1.43 as the game's first tank with several turrets, and with it came a system for controlling each turret on its own. Out of the box you do not have to think about it: the secondary turrets track wherever the main turret points, and pulling the trigger fires every gun able to bear on your aim point. Getting the most out of these tanks, though, means knowing how to pick guns individually.

## Reading the turret markers

Each turret gets a small circle on screen showing where it is aimed. If a circle turns red, that turret cannot fire at the angle you are aiming.

## Choosing which gun to fire

You can pick a weapon from the multifunction menu (Y by default) under Weaponry, using "Select primary weapon", "Select secondary weapon" or "Select machine gun", or use their keybinds. These are the binds worth setting, all under Controls → Ground vehicles → Weaponry:

| Bind | What it does |
|---|---|
| Fire from secondary guns | Fires the secondary guns |
| Select secondary weapon | Cycles through every turret, the main one included |
| Reset weapon selection | Drops your current choice and goes back to the default, all-turret setup |

"Select machine gun" cycles through the machine guns the same way "Select secondary weapon" cycles through the turrets. After you fire, reload indicators appear for both the main and the secondary guns.

## Firing everything at once

In the default all-turret mode, pressing your normal fire key only fires the main gun unless "Fire from secondary guns" is bound to **the same key**. Put both on one key if you want a full broadside every time you click.

## The range-scale trap

In the default mode, the sight uses the main gun's range scale for every turret. On the T-35 that means sniper mode is set up for the low-velocity 76 mm main gun, not the higher-velocity 45 mm guns, so at range the 45 mm shots will sail past the target. When you need those guns to hit at distance, select them on their own rather than relying on the all-turret default.

## Before your first battle

Set up the binds, then take the tank into a test drive and check that each gun fires when and how you expect. It is much cheaper to find a missing bind there than in a match.
