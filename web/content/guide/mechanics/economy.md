War Thunder's economy is really two systems running side by side. One is money: Silver Lions you earn, Golden Eagles you buy, and Warbonds from the Battle Pass. The other is progress, measured in research points (RP), which come in several kinds. This page covers both, plus the multipliers that speed them up or slow them down.

## Silver Lions (SL)

Silver Lions are the everyday currency, and almost everything you do costs some.

**Earning them.** Most SL comes from battles. How much depends on the game mode, how well and how actively you played, and whether your team won. You also get SL from achievements and battle awards, single-player missions, wagers and challenges, and you can buy SL with Golden Eagles.

**Multipliers.** Every vehicle has its own SL multiplier (and RP multiplier), shown on its card, and a higher multiplier means bigger rewards. Premium vehicles have double the multipliers of regular ones. A [premium account](/guide/mechanics/premium-account/) adds at least 50% to SL, and SL boosters give a temporary bonus. Our [economy table](/economy/) lists SL earnings per vehicle.

**Spending them.**

- Buying a vehicle once you've researched it, and training a crew for it
- Buying each modification after you've researched it
- Repairs after a battle, plus restocking ammunition and suspended weapons, and replacing crew during a battle
- Crew slots 3 to 5
- Raising a crew to Expert on a particular vehicle
- Creating training squadrons

## Golden Eagles (GE)

Golden Eagles are the paid currency. You never need them to progress, but they can speed things up and buy extras. They are sold on the Gaijin Store in bundles from 150 to 25,000, and they occasionally turn up as prizes, for example for placing well in tournaments.

You can spend GE on:

- certain premium vehicles
- a premium account
- speeding up vehicle research, via convertible RP (see below)
- modifications (bought this way, they don't then need to be bought with SL)
- crew experience points
- crew slots 6 to 10
- Ace qualification for a crew on a particular vehicle
- some cosmetics: camouflages, decals, decorators and naval ensigns
- Silver Lions
- the premium track of the Battle Pass, and Battle Pass progress
- completing stages of some events
- battle trophies with special prizes
- the right to found a squadron

Check [Gaijin Market prices](/market/) and current [deals](/deals/) before spending.

## Warbonds (WB)

You earn Warbonds by reaching certain Battle Pass levels and spend them in the Warbond shop. The shop's stock changes when each new Battle Pass season starts.

## Research points (RP)

RP is earned in battle on the same basis as SL: mode, performance, activity and result. It is boosted by the vehicle's RP multiplier, a premium account (+100% on all earnings), RP boosters, and talismans. A talisman on a vehicle gives +100% RP, but not SL, for that vehicle.

### Skill Bonus

On top of that, you get an extra RP multiplier for destroying enough opponents in one battle. In naval battles it is based on damage dealt instead.

| Mode | Small bonus | Medium bonus | Full bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air AB | 3–5 destroyed: +15% | 6–9: +40% | 10 or more: +100% |
| Air RB | 2 destroyed: +35% | 3: +75% | 4 or more: +100% |
| Ground AB and RB | 3–5 destroyed: +15% | 6–8: +50% | 9 or more: +100% |
| Ground SB | 3–4 destroyed: +20% | 5–7: +50% | 8 or more: +100% |
| Naval AB and RB | 6,500–9,999 damage: +20% | 10,000–14,999: +45% | 15,000 or more: +100% |

### Vehicle RP

This is what unlocks new vehicles. Everything you play from one branch of a nation's tree feeds a single research pool. How much of it actually lands depends on research efficiency:

- **Direct predecessor bonus.** Researching a vehicle with the one joined to it by an arrow in the tree gives 130% RP in Arcade and 110% in Realistic and Simulator.
- **Rank gap penalty.** Researching with a vehicle of a much higher or lower rank costs you RP, as shown below.
- The direct predecessor bonus wins over any rank gap penalty when the two vehicles are more than one rank apart.

| Rank gap | Using a lower-rank vehicle to research a higher-rank one | Using a higher-rank vehicle to research a lower-rank one |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No penalty | No penalty |
| 1 | No penalty | −10% |
| 2 | −60% | −70% |
| 3 | −70% | −90% |
| 4 to 7 | −80% | −95% |

Plan your research path with the [research planner](/research/).

**Vehicle Research Bonus.** Once you have researched and bought a "top" vehicle in a branch of a nation's tree, every vehicle in that branch, in that nation and in other nations, earns a bonus multiplier on vehicle RP. How big the bonus is depends on the branch and on the rank of the vehicle being researched. It applies to up to 5 battles per nation per branch per day, resetting at midnight GMT. A vehicle counts as "top" only if it is:

- researchable (not a squadron, premium, pack, event or hidden vehicle)
- last in its line (if the line ends in a group, the first vehicle of the group counts)
- at the tree's maximum rank
- no more than 2 BR steps below the tree's highest BR, taking its highest BR across all modes. For example, if the tree tops out at 13.7, only vehicles at 13.0 or above qualify.

### Modification RP

Each vehicle you take into battle earns RP for its own modifications. The amount equals the vehicle RP it earned before research efficiency is applied.

Finishing every modification in a tier pays out vehicle RP equal to 20% of that tier's total cost. A tier of five modifications costing 3,000 RP each (15,000 RP in all), for instance, would pay out 3,000 RP. That's a few hundred RP at low ranks but thousands at the top. You get it once for each of the four tiers, the first time you complete it, and it isn't changed by research efficiency, premium account or any other multiplier.

### Convertible RP

Convertible RP builds up in a separate pool that you can put towards any researchable vehicle. Converting costs 1 GE per 45 RP: 45 convertible RP becomes 45 vehicle RP. You earn the same amount as your vehicle and modification RP, minus any Skill Bonus. Some achievements and challenges award it too.

### Crew experience (Crew XP)

Crew XP pays for [crew skills](/guide/mechanics/crew-skills/). Each crew earns a share of the RP that its own vehicle earned:

| Mode | Crew XP per 1,000 RP |
|---|---|
| Arcade | 30 |
| Realistic | 11.3 |
| Simulator | 9 |

### Squadron RP

Squadron vehicles are researched with squadron RP. You don't earn it directly in battle. Instead, every 200 RP you earn adds one activity point to your squadron's shared pool. Every 3 days the pool is turned into RP for the squadron vehicle you have selected, and your share depends on how active you were compared with the rest of the squadron and on how big the pool is. Each player can add up to 360 activity points per 3-day period, and the pool holds at most 20,000.
