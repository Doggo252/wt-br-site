Some ground vehicles can spot enemies for the whole team and get paid for doing it. The scouting ability belongs to light tanks from rank II upwards, a small number of tank destroyers, and vehicles armed only with anti-aircraft guided missiles. You can find the light tanks for each nation in the [ground tech trees](/ground/).

## Using Active Scouting

Active Scouting is bound to **V** by default. To change it, go to the controls, open the Tank tab and look under Miscellaneous. How you aim it depends on the mode:

| Mode | How to scout | What your team gets |
|---|---|---|
| Arcade | Lock on to the enemy with the Lock Target key (in the Common controls), then press the scouting key | The enemy's marker stays visible and selectable, in the world and on the minimap, for 30 seconds |
| Realistic | Look through your binoculars or gunsight (Sniper Mode), put the crosshair right on the enemy, then press the scouting key | A small red arrow over the enemy, refreshed every so often, with its minimap position updating too, for 30 seconds |

Two rules to keep in mind:

- **One scout per target.** After a vehicle has been scouted, nobody can scout it again until 30 seconds have passed since that successful attempt.
- **Don't spam it in Realistic.** If you press the key without a hostile ground vehicle under your crosshair, the ability goes on a long cooldown.

## What scouting earns

A successful scout pays a little Silver Lions and Research Points on the spot. If a teammate then destroys that vehicle inside the 30-second window, you are paid again, and this second payment is the bigger one. You also receive the Intelligence award, while the player who got the kill receives According to Intelligence. Some battle tasks ask for these awards.

A kill on your scouted target also gives you a bonus inside the match:

- **Arcade:** one extra artillery strike and one air strike point. This only applies if the enemy wasn't already plainly visible to your teammate before you scouted it or before it died, or if the teammate who killed it is in your squad.
- **Realistic:** every aircraft in your lineup gets 7% cheaper in spawn points (SP), or 14% cheaper if you have the Airstrike modification.

## Helping teammates repair

Any vehicle with the Parts modification can help a teammate repair, but scouts do it better.

- A teammate who is already repairing shows a white wrench inside a timer circle. Park close to them and hold the Tank repair key. With a scout, their repair runs three times as fast; other vehicles only double it.
- A teammate who hasn't unlocked Parts can't repair at all on their own, so they show a plain white wrench to ask for help. You can fix them, but it takes the full repair time with no speed-up.

## Scout modifications

Scouting vehicles get two extra modifications, and each works differently by mode.

| Modification | Arcade | Realistic |
|---|---|---|
| Airstrike | You can always join an air battle that's already running as an Attacker or Bomber, on top of the normal Fighter option. For every extra Attacker or Bomber you bring, the enemy team may spawn one more Fighter. | Doubles the aircraft SP discount you get when a teammate kills your scouted target, from 7% to 14% |
| Improved Optics | Enemy markers start showing at a longer range | Your crew occasionally hints at an enemy with a small red arrow at the edge of the screen; this pushes out the range at which that happens |

In both modes, Improved Optics is essentially a boost to how far your crew can spot.
