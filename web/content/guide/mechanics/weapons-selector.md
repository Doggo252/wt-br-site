title: Weapons Selector
summary: How to bind and use the weapons selector to choose which guns, bombs, rockets or missiles your aircraft or helicopter fires next.

Normally every weapon type on an aircraft has its own key, and the game decides the order things come off the rails. Carry two kinds of air-to-air missile and you have to empty one kind before you can fire the other. The weapons selector, added in the "Raining Fire" update, puts you in charge: you choose which primary weapon and which secondary weapon are active, and two general fire keys shoot whatever is selected.

- It works for aircraft and helicopters only, not for tanks or ships.
- It is a separate system from the countermeasures selector, with its own controls.

## When it helps

- **Mixed missile loadouts.** Different missiles do very different jobs, and without the selector you can't choose which one goes first.
- **Mixed bombs.** Drop the smaller bomb first and keep the big one for a better target.
- **Mixed guns.** Fire only the cannons or only the machine guns so that one type of ammunition lasts longer.

## Binding the controls

Take your time here. If you bind these badly, you can end up unable to fire anything.

| Control | What it does | Sensible binding |
|---|---|---|
| Fire primary weapon | Fires the selected primary weapon, whatever type it is. Works only in selector mode. | The key you already use for guns (left mouse button by default) |
| Fire secondary weapon | Fires the selected secondary weapon, whatever type it is. Works only in selector mode. | The same key as one of your usual bomb, rocket or missile keys |
| Switch primary weapons | Steps through your primary weapons and turns selector mode on | Somewhere you can reach easily in flight |
| Switch secondary weapons | Steps through your secondary weapons and turns selector mode on | Somewhere you can reach easily in flight |
| Exit selected weapon mode | Turns selector mode off and gives you your normal keys back | Anything convenient |

Keep your existing per-weapon keys (Small-calibre guns, Large-calibre guns, Additional guns, Drop bomb, Fire rocket, Fire air-to-ground missile, Fire air-to-air missile). They are what you use when the selector is off. While the selector is on, each one only works when its own weapon type is the selected one, alongside the general fire keys.

There is also an option in the settings that turns selector mode on automatically at the start of every mission.

## Using it in flight

Press either Switch key and the selector comes on. You'll see:

- "Weapon selection on" at the bottom of the screen
- a ">" next to the selected primary weapon and the selected secondary weapon in your weapon list
- the name of the selected weapon, if you carry more than one weapon of the same kind (two missile types or two bomb sizes, say)

For a mixed group, the count shows two numbers. On a Phantom FGR.2 carrying both Skyflash and AIM-9G Sidewinder missiles, "8/4" means eight air-to-air missiles on board, four of which are the selected Skyflashes.

**The catch:** while the selector is on, anything that isn't selected is locked out, even on its own key. If missiles are selected and you want to bomb, press Switch secondary weapons until the ">" sits next to the bombs. To get the old behaviour back, press Exit selected weapon mode.

On the primary side, selecting all primary weapons makes Fire primary weapon shoot every gun at once. That includes upward-firing Schräge Musik guns, whether or not Schräge Musik mode is on. Defensive gunners you control from Gunner View are not affected by the selector.

## Examples

- **Phantom FGR.2** with four Skyflash, four AIM-9G, two 1,000 lb bombs, three 540 lb bombs and SNEB rockets. Without the selector, every Sidewinder has to go before the first Skyflash, though bombs and rockets can be released at any time on their own keys. With the selector you pick the missile, but you also have to cycle to the bombs or rockets before you can use them. The 20 mm gun pod is the only primary weapon, so it is always selected.
- **P-47D-28.** It normally drops its two 1,000 lb bombs before its 500 lb bomb. The selector lets you drop the 500 lb bomb first. While the selector is on, its rockets fire from Fire secondary weapon once selected, not from Fire rocket.
- **Spitfire Mk IIb.** It has 7.7 mm machine guns and 20 mm cannons. You can set Fire primary weapon to shoot just one group or all of them.
