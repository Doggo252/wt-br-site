In high-tier ground battles, a player who builds up enough airstrike points (Arcade) or spawn points (Realistic) can take off in an aircraft carrying a nuclear bomb. Drop it successfully and the battle ends there and then, as a win for the team that dropped first.

## What you need

| | Ground Arcade | Ground Realistic |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | 15 airstrike points | 2,500 spawn points |
| Battle rating | 6.0 and up | Carriers listed from 6.7 (see below) |

In Arcade, you earn airstrike points by destroying enemy tanks and aircraft yourself, or in other ways such as scouting, and you have to reach the total without calling in a bomber along the way. Nuclear bombers aren't available in Arcade battles where helicopters have been picked at random as the air support.

## How the rules changed

| When | Change |
|---|---|
| Update "New Power", 17 November 2020 | Introduced in Ground Arcade only, from BR 7.3, for 20 airstrike points |
| Patch of 19 November, two days later | Opened up to BR 6.0 and above |
| Update "Hot Tracks" | Cost cut from 20 to 15 airstrike points |
| Update "Ixwa Strike", 10 March 2021 | Added to Ground Realistic for 3,000 spawn points |
| Patch of 19 March, nine days later | Realistic cost cut to 2,500 spawn points |

## Carrier aircraft in Realistic

| Battle rating | Aircraft |
|---|---|
| 6.7–7.7 | Tu-4, B-29A-BN, Washington B.Mk.I |
| 8.0–9.0 | IL-28, Canberra B Mk 6 |
| 9.3–11.0 | Su-7BKL, Jaguar A |
| 11.3–12.7 | Su-24M, F-111F, Tornado IDS ASSTA1 |
| 13.0 and up | F-16A ADF, F-16A MLU, F-16D Barak II, F-2A, MiG-29SMT (9-19) |

In the game these aircraft carry a radiation symbol in front of their names. They differ from the normal versions in two ways: you can't swap the nuclear bomb for another payload (you can still change gun belts), and they carry no national markings, because each one is shared between several nations.

## Background

Gaijin had hinted at player-launched nukes for a while. The 2018 April Fools event "Silent Thunder" had players capture points with submarines to unlock missile launches, some of them implied to be nuclear, which won the match for the capturing side. The "New Power" teaser ended in a huge flash. On launch day, 17 November 2020, players saw a siren and countdown end in an explosion that forced a game restart. They came back to the new hangar, with a nuclear blast on the horizon marking the end of the old one.
