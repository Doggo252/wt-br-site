On many aircraft and helicopters you are not stuck with the preset weapon packages: you can fill each pylon yourself and save the result as your own loadout. The editor arrived with the "It's fixed No. 51" update. You will find it mostly on higher-rank jets and helicopters, though a few lower-rank aircraft have it too, and more vehicles get it with each major update. Browse the [aircraft](/air/) and [helicopter](/helicopters/) trees to find something to try it on.

## Opening the editor

1. In the hangar, right-click the aircraft.
2. Open the "Secondary Weapons" menu.
3. Under the custom loadouts editor, click "Create".

## Filling the pylons

Right-click a pylon slot and pick the weapon you want for it, then work your way across the aircraft. Three limits keep the build honest:

- **Total mass.** The maximum weight of suspended weapons is shown in the middle of the screen. Go over it and the game will not let you save.
- **Balance.** The two sides do not have to match exactly, but there is a limit to how lopsided the load can be. The game warns you when you cross it.
- **Compatibility.** Some weapons block the pylons next to them, or need extra equipment fitted before they can be carried. When that happens a window pops up explaining the clash. On the Su-24M, for example, carrying the KAB-500Kr-M rules out the Kh-29TE. Elsewhere, the Aspide missile can only be fitted together with the Aspide aim block and with the M61A1 cannon removed.

## Where to hang what

Most aircraft follow the same pattern: the biggest stores on the centreline under the fuselage, medium ones under the wings and the lightest on the wingtip pylons. Think a 3000 lb bomb in the middle, 1000 lb bombs under the wings and air-to-air missiles on the tips.

Mixed builds are where the editor shines. A JAS-39C, for instance, can go up with three AGM-65G, an R-Darter, two AIM-9M, a BOL countermeasure pod and a LITENING II targeting pod all at once, covering ground attack and self-defence in a single sortie.
