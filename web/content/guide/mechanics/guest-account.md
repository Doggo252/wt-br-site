A guest account lets you try War Thunder straight away, without first registering a Gaijin.Net account. It is a full game account in most respects, but it is tied to your device, so treat it as a way to get started and upgrade it once you know you're staying.

## Making one

When the game client asks you to log in:

1. Choose "Guest Login".
2. Type the nickname you want. If someone already has it, the game adds numbers to the end.

That's all. There's no time limit on a guest account, and you can play every game mode, research vehicles and earn in-game currency.

## What you give up

- **Your progress is only as safe as your device.** If the device breaks you can lose your progress, and reinstalling your operating system can lock you out of the account.
- **One per system:** you can create one guest account per operating system install.
- **Chat** has some restrictions.
- **No store purchases.** You can't buy Golden Eagles, a Premium Account or premium vehicle packs from the Gaijin.Net store.
- **No other services.** You can't log in to the official forums, the wiki, War Thunder Live and other Gaijin services.

Guest accounts still get an internal ID just like registered ones. Reports against them work the same way, and they can be banned for breaking the rules or using forbidden modifications.

## Turning it into a full account

You can bind an email address at any time. That confirms the account and turns it into a normal Gaijin.Net account with no restrictions.

1. In the hangar, click your nickname, just to the left of "To Battle!", to open your Player Card.
2. Press "Email Bind".
3. In the in-game browser, enter an email address that you can get into and that isn't already registered with Gaijin.Net.
4. Open the email you receive and click the confirmation link.
5. Choose a strong password, enter it in the window that opens, and press "Link".

Once that's done you can change your password whenever you like, log in from more than one device, chat without limits and buy from the Gaijin.Net store, with your progress no longer tied to one machine.
