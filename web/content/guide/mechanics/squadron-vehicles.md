Squadron vehicles are special vehicles you can't research the normal way. You unlock them with squadron Research Points, which you earn just by playing while you are in a squadron, or you can buy them with Golden Eagles. They usually have something unusual going for them, like the M901's missile launcher or the Firecrest's torpedo.

## How squadron research works

Your normal battles feed a shared pool:

1. **Earn activity.** Every 200 Research Points you earn in battle adds one Activity Point to your squadron's pool.
2. **Wait for the conversion.** Every 3 days, the pool is turned into Research Points for the squadron vehicle you have selected. How much you get depends on how active you were compared with your squadmates, and on how many points are in the pool overall.
3. **Mind the caps.** You can put in at most 360 points in each 3-day period, and the squadron's pool holds no more than 20,000 points per period.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Research Points per Activity Point | 200 |
| Conversion period | Every 3 days |
| Your contribution per 3-day period | Up to 360 points |
| Squadron pool per 3-day period | Up to 20,000 points |

## Good to know

- **Any rank works.** You can research any squadron vehicle while playing a vehicle of any rank.
- **Progress isn't lost when you leave.** If you quit your squadron, your squadron vehicle research picks up again as soon as you join any other squadron.
- **Checking activity.** To see a member's activity for the current three days, right-click their name in the squadron member list and choose "activity".
- **Buying with Golden Eagles.** You can skip the research and buy a squadron vehicle outright. The price goes down to reflect the Research Points you already have on it.
