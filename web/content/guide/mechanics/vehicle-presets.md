Vehicle presets let you save more than one lineup for a nation and swap between them with a click. Instead of rebuilding your crew slots every time you move from naval to jets, or from one battle rating to another, you set each lineup up once and recall it whenever you need it. Our [lineup finder](/lineup/) can help you work out which vehicles belong together in the first place.

## Where to find them

The "Presets" button sits below the crew slot panel. Next to it are buttons labelled with the names of your existing presets, so you can jump straight to any of them.

## Filling a preset

There are two ways to put a vehicle into a crew slot:

- **From the crew slots.** Click an empty slot and choose a vehicle from the menu that pops up.
- **From the research tree.** Find the vehicle, click it and press "Select Crew", or simply drag it onto the slot you want.

To take a vehicle out, drag it onto the "Remove vehicle from preset" area, or click "change vehicle" and then "Remove from preset".

### Arranging the slots

You can drag crew slots around to put vehicles in whatever order suits you. Grab a slot by its lower edge, where the slot number and crew level are shown, and drop it on another slot to swap them. Swapping is free and unlimited. Each preset keeps its own order, so rearranging one leaves the others untouched.

## The presets menu

Click "Presets" to open the menu. Your current preset is saved automatically and shows up here.

The left side lists the presets for the nation you are looking at, up to fourteen of them. The right side shows the vehicles in whichever preset you have selected. Along the bottom are the controls:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Add | Creates a new preset |
| Copy | Makes an exact duplicate of a preset |
| Rename | Gives a preset its own name so it is easier to tell apart |
| Arrow buttons | Move a preset up or down the list |
| Delete | Removes a preset for good; there is no undo. You cannot delete your only preset or the one currently in use |
| Apply | Switches to the selected preset. If you select the one already in use, the button reads "Close" instead |

## Example: one preset for ships, one for jets

1. Fill your first preset with mid-rank ships for Realistic Naval Battles.
2. Open the presets menu and click "Add". A new "Preset #2" appears, holding a single vehicle with the maximum battle rating.
3. Click "Apply" to switch to it, then fill its slots with your jets for Air Realistic Battles.
4. Reopen the menu and rename the two presets, say "Navy" and "Jets", then reorder them however you like.

From then on, the buttons below the crew slots let you hop between the two lineups in a moment.
