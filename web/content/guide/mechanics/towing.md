Ground vehicles and ships can hook a cable between each other so that one can pull the other. It is the way to rescue a teammate who is stuck, flipped or crippled, and with a little practice you can use it to set an overturned tank back on its tracks.

## The key

Towing is bound to **0** by default. You'll find it in the controls under Miscellaneous, as "Towing rope". Both players use the same key: the one who wants to be pulled, and the one doing the pulling.

## How to tow

1. **The stuck player asks for a tow** by pressing the key. A white tow hook appears above their vehicle so teammates know they are ready.
2. **The rescuer drives up close** and points their cursor at the stuck vehicle. A preview of the rope appears, and it attaches wherever the cursor is, so you choose the hook point.
3. **The rescuer presses the key.** The rope locks on at that point and the two vehicles are linked. Now drive off and pull.

## Where to hook the rope

Because the rope attaches where you aim, the hook point decides what happens:

- **At the centre of gravity:** the vehicle is simply dragged along the ground.
- **Near one end or edge:** the vehicle swings round.
- **High up:** the vehicle tips over.

That last one is how you right a flipped tank:

- **On its side:** drive round to the underside, hook on to the tracks sticking up in the air, and drive away from it.
- **On its side with the underside against an obstacle:** first push or tow it clear of the obstacle with your hull, then pull.
- **Upside down but still able to move:** hook on to the far side and pull hard enough to roll it back over.

## Tips

- **Power and weight matter.** A light, weak vehicle can't move a heavy one. The 6.4-ton Panzerjäger I with its 100-horsepower engine can't usefully tow a 21-ton Panzer IV E, and a small boat will struggle with a destroyer.
- **Pull along the tracks.** A tracked vehicle is much easier to drag forwards or backwards than sideways. Ships are the same: pulling towards the bow or stern beats pulling them broadside.
- **A fast vehicle can tow a slow one**, provided it isn't too light. The towed vehicle can even go faster than its own top speed. It's rarely worth it, but it can get a wheeled vehicle, or one with a poor power-to-weight ratio, up a hill.
- **Answer the hook icon.** When a teammate gets hit and switches on the tow signal, they are usually asking to be dragged behind hard cover. Their driver, transmission or engine is probably knocked out, so they can't back out of enemy fire on their own. Pulling them clear can decide the whole fight.

## When towing doesn't work

A vehicle can't be towed while:

- its tracks are broken
- its crew is being replenished
- its turret is clipped into the ground after a flip
