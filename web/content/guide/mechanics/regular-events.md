Since February 2024, War Thunder has run a steady stream of in-game events, one after another with hardly a gap, some of them timed to holidays. Each event is built around a single vehicle, which is the main reward. Tradable coupons for these vehicles are bought and sold on the Gaijin Market; see our [Market prices](/market/) if you'd rather buy than grind.

## Earning the rewards

Rewards are unlocked with mission points from battles. You need between 35,000 and 45,000 mission points every two days. The points you earn are multiplied according to the game mode and the highest rank of the vehicles you used in that battle.

To see how far along you are, open your profile, go to the Achievements tab, pick the current event and click the reward you're after. It shows how many tasks that reward needs and how many you've done.

## Claiming a reward

1. When you have enough marks of distinction for a reward, a notice about uncollected rewards appears under your profile icon, next to the "To Battle" button.
2. Open your profile, go to Achievements, choose the event, and collect the reward with its button.

What you receive is a **non-tradable coupon** for the vehicle.

## Making the coupon tradable

To sell the vehicle instead of keeping it, you need the **Coupon Upgrade** item.

1. **Earn it.** The Coupon Upgrade comes from reaching a set number of mission points during the event. You can see the target under your profile, Achievements, the event name, then "Coupon Upgrade".
2. **Collect it.** Like the coupon, it has to be picked up once you've earned it.
3. **Use it in time.** If you neither use it nor exchange it for bonds, it disappears after the period given in its description.
4. **Upgrade the coupon.** In your inventory, select the vehicle coupon and press "Modify".

You now have a tradable coupon. You can activate it on your own account or sell it on the Market, but selling only becomes possible 6 days after the upgrade.
