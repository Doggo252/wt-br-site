Every battle you fight feeds experience into your account as a whole, not only into the vehicles you took out. That running total decides your account level, which stops at 100. Some of the levels along the way also hand you a new in-game rank, and each level you gain moves you through the stages of the "Only the Stars Are Higher" achievement.

This is your player level, not the rank of a vehicle in a tech tree.

## How the curve is shaped

- **The early levels fly by.** Level 1 needs only 500 XP, and you hit level 10 at 245,600 XP.
- **After level 10 the cost rises slowly and steadily.** Level 10 to 11 costs 77,000 XP, and every later step costs 1,800 to 1,900 XP more than the one before it, ending at 239,400 XP for level 99 to 100.
- **The top is a long way off.** Level 100 sits at 14,482,500 XP in total. Level 50 (4,748,700 XP) is only about a third of the way there in raw experience.
