Once you own a top vehicle of one type, the game helps you research that same type in every other nation. Each day, every nation gets its own research bonus for up to 5 battles per vehicle type: ground vehicles, helicopters, aircraft, bluewater ships and coastal ships. Plan which nation to push with our [research planner](/research/).

## How the bonus works

- **Unlock condition:** you need to have researched and bought a "top" vehicle of that type in any nation (see below).
- **Size:** the bonus is calculated from the base Research Points you earn in a battle, the same way skill bonuses work, and shows in the battle results. How big it is depends on the tree type and the vehicle's rank; hover over the research bonus icon on a vehicle you're researching to see the figures.
- **Reset:** the 5 bonus battles refresh every day at 00:00 GMT. The game shows that time converted to your local time.
- **Where it applies:** any random battle mode, provided the vehicle you used is eligible.

The icon on a vehicle only appears if that vehicle type already has research bonuses unlocked. It's lit up only if you still have bonus battles left today and the vehicle isn't classed as new.

## What counts as a "top" vehicle

A vehicle has to meet every one of these conditions:

1. **Researchable.** Premium, squadron, event, store pack, Marketplace and hidden vehicles don't count.
2. **Last in its line** of the research tree. If the last spot is a group (folder), the first vehicle in the group counts.
3. **At the tree's highest rank.**
4. **Close to the tree's top BR.** Its highest Battle Rating in any mode must be no more than 2 BR steps below the highest BR in that tree. For example, if a tree tops out at 13.7, two steps down is 13.0 (13.7, then 13.3, then 13.0), so any vehicle below 13.0 doesn't qualify even if it sits at the top rank.

In the tree, top vehicles are joined by an arrow to the Research Bonus block at the bottom. If at least one of those arrows isn't red, the bonus is unlocked for that tree.

## Keeping track

Hover over the Research Bonus block in the ground, aircraft, helicopter, bluewater or coastal tree to see which nations still have bonus battles left today. Each nation also shows a daily research bonus icon; hovering over it tells you which vehicle the bonus will go to.

If you have top vehicles in lots of trees, that list gets long. You can hide it for trees you're not working on by switching off the "Show the remaining bonus in headers" option.

After a battle, any bonus you received appears in the My results screen under "Vehicle research bonus", and in the Battles section of your messages.

## Special cases

- **Enduring Confrontation:** every 30 minutes in battle uses up one bonus battle, the same way boosters are consumed. The bonus is still based on that match's base RP, so using several bonus battles in one match doesn't add anything. With 5 bonus battles left, a 3-hour EC match pays the same bonus as one bonus battle would.
- **Several vehicle types in one battle:** if you spawn, say, a plane and a tank in a combined battle and have bonuses for both, each vehicle's base reward gets its bonus, and a bonus battle is used for both aircraft and ground vehicles.
- **Battles where you earn nothing:** if a vehicle gets 0 mission points and 0 base RP (for example, you took a helicopter into Ground Realistic and the battle ended before you could do anything), that type gets no bonus and its bonus battle isn't used up.

## Common questions

**I only play the USSR, but I own top-rank aircraft, ground vehicles and helicopters there. How many bonuses do I get?**
Five bonus battles a day for each nation in each of those types: 5 for American aircraft, 5 for the American ground tree, 5 for American helicopters, and the same for every other nation.

**I have every US aircraft but US ground only up to rank IV. Do I get ground bonuses?**
No. A top vehicle only unlocks bonuses for the same vehicle type. A top aircraft gives you aircraft bonuses in other nations, not ground bonuses.

**Can I hide the bonus icons for nations I don't play?**
Yes, with the "Show remaining bonus in headers" option in the research tree.

**What if a new vehicle is added above my only top vehicle?**
Bonus status is recalculated whenever new vehicles arrive. If your only top vehicle stops being the top of its line, the bonus disappears until you buy the new vehicle or get another top vehicle.

**Can I use the bonus to research the new top vehicle?**
Yes, but not straight away. The bonus doesn't apply to a vehicle while it's still marked as new.
