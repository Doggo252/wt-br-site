Dual control means a vehicle has a second set of controls for a key job, so one crewman can step in for another. Usually that is a commander who can work the gun, though on some vehicles it is a gunner who can drive. The point is survival. When someone is knocked out, a crewmate who already has the controls picks up the job straight away instead of losing time moving into the empty seat. The mechanic was added in Update 2.7 "Red Skies".

## How it works

Dual control is passive; you do not have to do anything. If the gunner is knocked out, the commander takes over the gun automatically, as long as the commander is alive. And if you happen to be looking through the commander's sight at the moment the gunner goes down, there is no delay at all.

The commander taking over the gun is the most common case, but not the only one:

- On the **Sd.Kfz.234**, the machine gunner can drive if the driver is lost.
- On the **Strv 103**, the commander can both fire and drive.

## "Double" mode: shooting from the commander's sight

If a vehicle has both commander fire control and a commander's sight, the commander can fire through his own sight even while the gunner is perfectly healthy. You need two keybinds for this:

| Function | Where to bind it |
|---|---|
| Switch to the commander's sight | Controls → Ground vehicles → Camera control → Commander sight |
| Turn on "Double" mode | Controls → Ground vehicles → Weaponry → Commander fire control |

Once it is on, "AIM Mode" appears in the top-left corner of the screen and the commander has control of the gun.

## Why bother

- **Better view.** The commander's sight often has a wider field of view and sits higher on the vehicle. That lets you, for instance, guide ATGMs while the rest of the vehicle stays behind cover.
- **Better optics.** It usually comes with night vision or a thermal imager.
- **Easier long-range shots.** Most modern tanks also have a laser rangefinder with automatic range correction.

## Mind the offset

The commander's sight is not lined up with the gun, so you need to allow for the gap. On the Black Night, for example, the sight is on the right-hand side, so aim slightly to the right when firing at an enemy tank.
