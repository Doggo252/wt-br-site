War Thunder has been protected by the BattlEye anti-cheat since December 2024. Most banned modifications are now stopped as soon as you launch the game. BattlEye works from two ends, on the servers and on your PC, watching for anything that interferes with the game while it runs. On top of that, Gaijin runs its own detection and a player report system.

## How the protection works

If BattlEye detects a banned modification, it blocks you from joining battles. For everyone else it stays out of the way: apart from an installation prompt at launch, you won't notice it.

It replaced Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC), which the game had used since update 1.91 "Night Vision".

Alongside BattlEye, Gaijin uses its own system. It combines player reports, review of battle replays, and heuristic analysis of how each player behaves in battle. It is designed to spot players who are using information the game client is supposed to keep to itself.

## Turning it on and off

The game launcher has an "Anticheat" switch. You can turn it off, but you then can't join battles in the modes and Battle Ratings that require it.

| Mode | Anti-cheat required |
|---|---|
| Modes without markers, such as Ground Realistic, Ground Simulator and Air Simulator | Yes |
| Air Arcade, Air Realistic, Ground Arcade | For players and squads whose lineup has a Battle Rating of 4.7 or higher |

Originally it was only compulsory in the modes without markers. Modes with markers were left out because markers already show you where enemies are, which limits what a cheat can add. The It's Fixed №107 update extended the requirement to Air Arcade, Air Realistic and Ground Arcade from BR 4.7 up, because at high BR battles are fast and hard, and even a small advantage can decide them. Gaijin has said it plans to lower that BR threshold gradually.

## Reporting a player

The classic report system still works alongside the anti-cheat, backed by battle replays.

### During a battle

1. Open the scoreboard with **Tab**.
2. Right-click the player's name and choose "Report".
3. Pick the right complaint category.

### After a battle, from a replay

1. Open the replay service: in the game, go to Community, then Streams and Replays; or sign in to the replay service in your web browser with your game account.
2. Use the filters to narrow things down by mode, battle type or vehicle, or enter the player's name to list every battle they played.
3. Press "Search", find the right battle and press "Start replay".
4. Back in the game client, watch the replay and note the in-replay time when the violation happens.
5. On the site, press the "Complain" button (marked with an exclamation point) next to the player's name and choose a category.
6. In the "Complaint text" box, give all the details, including the replay time of the violation, then press "Complain".

The complaint goes for review straight away.

## Common questions

**Do sound mods still work?**
Yes.

**Will the anti-cheat lower my FPS?**
No. It doesn't affect frame rate in the game, and because BattlEye only runs while the War Thunder client does, it doesn't slow down your system or other programs either.

If the anti-cheat won't start or clashes with other software, report it through Gaijin's issue reporting service.
