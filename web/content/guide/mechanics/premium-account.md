A premium account is a paid membership that lasts for a set period. It doesn't unlock any vehicles. What it does is make everything you play earn more, and it adds a few conveniences. You can buy it in-game with Golden Eagles, or with real money on the Gaijin Store.

Don't confuse it with premium vehicles, which are bought separately and have their own boosted multipliers (see [premium vehicles](/premiums/)).

## Bigger rewards

| Reward | Premium bonus |
|---|---|
| Research Points, all game modes | +100% |
| Silver Lions for active actions in Random Battles | +50% |
| Silver Lions for time spent in battle, Random Battles | +100% |
| Silver Lions in other game modes | +50% |

Premium is one of several things that multiply your earnings. The others are each vehicle's own SL and RP multipliers, boosters and talismans. One reward it does not touch is the RP bonus for completing a whole tier of modifications, which stays the same with or without premium. See [Economy](/guide/mechanics/economy/) for how all of these fit together, and our [economy table](/economy/) for what each vehicle earns.

## No more losing money on a bad game

If automatic repair, automatic ammo restocking and Crew Replenishment would leave you with fewer Silver Lions than you started the battle with, premium covers those costs so the battle doesn't put you in the red.

## The other perks

- 4 decal slots
- ban one map from rotation in each game mode
- take loading screens out of rotation
- hide your real nickname in battle
- change the condition of a camouflage
