On PC you can play War Thunder in a virtual reality headset as well as on a monitor. Any mode can be played in VR, but flying gets the most out of it. You sit inside one of hundreds of detailed cockpits, and if you add a throttle and joystick, the result is close to a home flight simulator.

## Supported headsets

| Year or update | What changed |
|---|---|
| 2013 | VR support first added, for the original Oculus Rift |
| 2016 | Support extended to the HTC Vive and similar headsets |
| Update 2.7 "Red Skies" | VR mode moved to the OpenXR standard, with much better image quality and support for any compatible headset |

## Turning VR on

There are two ways in:

- **Game launcher:** turn on the "VR" switch before starting the game.
- **Steam:** when you launch, Steam offers to start the game in VR mode through SteamVR.

## Consoles

The console versions of War Thunder have no VR mode. Console players get a separate game instead: alongside the 2023 launch of PlayStation VR2, a VR spin-off for PC and PS5 was announced, and it came out on 3 February 2026 as Aces of Thunder. It is purely about air combat, in legendary aircraft over famous locations, and is sold on the PlayStation Store and Steam.
