---
title: Crew Lock
summary: When leaving a battle early locks your lineup, how long the lock lasts, and which vehicles are never affected.
source: https://wiki.warthunder.com/mechanics/183-crew-lock
---

Leave a match before you've used your lineup and the game locks it. Locked crew slots can't join another battle, you can't swap vehicles in or out of them, and you can't switch to a different preset until the lock runs out. Crew lock applies in every game mode.

## What triggers it

Any early exit counts, including ones that aren't really your fault:

- destroying your own vehicle, by crashing or by bailing out ("J-ing out")
- going back to the hangar while you still had vehicles you could spawn
- getting disconnected, whether the game crashed or your connection dropped

## "Vehicles you could still spawn"

Only two kinds of vehicle count towards a lock: ones you had enough spawn points for, and ones you hadn't spawned yet in that match. Once every vehicle you could afford has been out once, you can leave freely, even if a backup would have let you spawn one again.

There is one trap. If a vehicle was only too expensive because of its loadout, and you could have brought its spawn cost down by changing its weapons during the battle, the game treats it as spawnable. Leaving then earns you a lock.

## How long it lasts

The lock runs for nine minutes counted from the **start of the match**, not from when you left. Leave after the nine-minute mark and there's no lock at all. Leave four minutes in and you wait about five.

A countdown above the lineup bar at the bottom of the screen shows how long is left. In the meantime you can switch to another nation whose crews aren't locked and keep playing.

## Vehicles that are never locked

- In modes with several spawns, any vehicle you used in that battle and lost normally.
- Vehicles that couldn't take part in that battle at all, either because they were being repaired or because they aren't allowed in that mode. An aircraft or a ship sitting in your lineup during a Ground Arcade battle is one example.
- Reserve vehicles, always.
