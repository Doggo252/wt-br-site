Every vehicle's crew gets better with experience. A trained crew spots enemies further away, reloads faster, repairs quicker and survives hits a green crew wouldn't. This page covers how crews earn experience, how qualifications work, what each skill does, and how much it costs to max them.

## Earning and spending crew experience

You earn crew experience points (crew XP) from the research points you earn in battle, at a rate that depends on the mode. In Realistic and Simulator, it works out at roughly 1 crew XP per 100 RP, all of it going to the crew of the vehicle you played. In Arcade you get 3 crew XP per 100 RP, but that larger amount is spread over several crews. (The exact rates per 1,000 RP are on the [Economy](/guide/mechanics/economy/) page.) You can also buy crew XP with Golden Eagles from the "Accelerated Training" tab at the top right of the Crew Training window.

In the hangar, each crew slot shows the crew's level in its bottom-left corner. Silver text means the crew has some unspent points. Glowing gold text means it has a lot.

To spend points, either open the info card's Crew tab (on the left of the hangar) and press "Crew Training", or select a crew and press "Crew". Then press "+" beside the skill you want. Every level costs more than the last.

## Qualifications

Skill points improve a crew in general, whatever it drives. Qualifications are different: they apply to **one specific vehicle** only. Expert is bought with Silver Lions and Ace with Golden Eagles. Each needs a minimum crew level first. Crew level is the sum of all the crew's skill levels, so a level 5 crew, for example, can't yet be qualified on the M6A1, a Rank III vehicle.

### Aircraft crews

For aircraft, skill points only take you halfway: level 5 out of 10.

| Stage | Skill level | Share of the maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Skill points only | 5 | 50% |
| Plus Expert | 8 (+3) | 80% |
| Plus Ace | 10 (+2 more) | 100% |

Keen Vision shows what that means in practice. At level 5 a pilot spots targets at about 6 km. Expert pushes that to 7.2 km, and Ace to the maximum of 8 km.

### Ground and naval crews

Skill points cover 40% of the way to the top level. The other 60% comes from the Expert and Ace qualifications and from the commander's Leadership skill.

## Skills shared by every crew

These are logistical skills, and they count whatever type of vehicle the crew is in.

- **Repair Speed:** speeds up repairs in the hangar, up to three times faster when maxed. It also speeds up repairs for aircraft that land at a friendly airfield during a battle.
- **Repair Rank:** sets the highest vehicle rank that Repair Speed works on. It has five stages:

| Repair Rank stage | Highest vehicle rank covered |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 8 |

## Aircraft crew skills

### Pilot and gunners

- **G-Tolerance:** raises the g-load the crew can take before the screen darkens or reddens and the pilot blacks out (G-LOC) and loses control for a while. In Mouse Aim it also affects how precisely the aircraft follows your inputs.
- **Stamina:** slows fatigue. As a sortie goes on, and the more g the crew pulls, accuracy and G-Tolerance slowly drop. This also affects aiming accuracy in Mouse Aim.
- **Vitality:** lowers the chance of a fatal wound. An untrained pilot will usually die from a second .303 hit (from an MG 15 or similar) or a similar shrapnel hit. A maxed one can, with luck, survive one hit from a .50 cal (12.7 mm) M2 Browning.

### Pilot only

- **Keen Vision:** the furthest distance at which an enemy's marker can appear, within a cone in the direction you're looking. It ranges from 4 km untrained to 8 km maxed (3–5 km in RB and SB). Target size, light, cloud, weather and other factors also play a part.
- **Awareness:** detection in a full sphere around the aircraft, wherever you are looking. It ranges from 400 m to 800 m (300–500 m in RB and SB).

### Gunners

- **Number of Experienced Gunners:** if the aircraft has more gunner positions than you have experienced gunners, all gunner skills are cut in proportion. An He 111 H-6 has 5 positions: with 2 experienced gunners their skills drop by 60%, with 4 by 20%, and with all 5 they work at full strength.
- **Fire Accuracy** (AI-controlled gunners only): faster turret traverse, bursts up to 3 times longer, and opening fire from up to 275% further away (55% in RB and SB). Gunners also anticipate a target's direction from further out, fire together and lead targets more often, and misjudge lead less (7 times less in RB and SB when maxed).
- **Fire Precision** (AI-controlled gunners only): 2.5 times less spread (2 times in RB and SB) and bursts up to a third longer. Gunners open fire slightly further away in Arcade (up to 8%), and fire together and lead targets more often, as with Fire Accuracy.

### Logistics

- **Reload Speed:** faster rearming in the air and on the airfield. Maxed, it is 3 times faster for cannons and machine guns, 2 times for bombs, rockets and torpedoes, and 4 times for turrets. On an La-5FN, for example, cannon rearming drops from 40 s to 13.3 s and bombs take 20 s instead of 40.
- **Weapon Maintenance:** primary weapons overheat more slowly and are up to 3 times less likely to jam from overheating. Bombs and rockets spread less, and bombs, rockets and torpedoes have up to 3 times less angular velocity, which makes them noticeably more accurate.

## Ground crew skills

### Every crew member

- **Keen Vision:** how far away you can see enemy vehicles, including ones partly behind cover. The server only tells your game about a concealed vehicle once you are close enough: about 563 m if you're looking straight at it with a crew that isn't maxed. Binoculars or the fire control view triple your direct spotting distance but narrow your view. In third person you can spot enemies within 235 m of your hull front and 97 m all around. Maxing the skill on the whole crew adds up to 63% to the base, and Ace adds another 63%, for a total of 222% vision effectiveness.
  - Scouting vehicles can fit the "Improved Optics" modification, which raises the skill's base power by 30% (at best 1,621 m without binoculars).
  - In Arcade, the skill also sets how far away enemy markers show through soft cover, whether you see an enemy's full name or just its class, and how much of an enemy must be exposed before it is marked. Maxed, it even lets you see enemies through solid walls at very short range when looking right at them. Detection isn't all-round, though: only line of sight and line of movement count, so keep swinging the camera around in towns and hills.
- **Field Repair:** up to 50% faster repairs of damaged parts, with the biggest effect on tracks. The crew's average level is what counts.
- **Agility:** how quickly another crew member takes over from one who has been knocked out. The crew average counts here too, so piling points onto one man gains nothing over spreading them.
- **Vitality:** lowers the chance of a fatal wound.

### Role skills

| Crew member | Skill | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | Tank Driving | Faster automatic gear changes and braking, so better acceleration and turning. A gear change normally takes 1 s; the skill cuts up to 0.36 s off that, and with other factors it can drop to 0.1 s. Braking reaction goes from 0.14 s to 0.11 s with the skill, and down to 0.07 s with other factors. |
| Gunner | Targeting | Speed and precision of traverse and elevation. An experienced gunner moves both axes at once, while an inexperienced one moves one at a time. It can also raise turret traverse speed, and on ATGM carriers it may make missiles respond better to your inputs. |
| Gunner | Rangefinding | How accurate the distance estimate is when you use the "Rangefinder" function |
| Commander | Leadership | Raises every crew member's skills by up to 10%. Qualifications don't change it, and it's among the costliest skills to train. |
| Loader | Weapon Reloading | Up to 23% faster loading; the skill alone, fully trained, gives about 11%. No effect on guns with autoloaders. |
| Radio operator | Artillery Strike Calling Time | Delay before the shells fire drops from 13 s to as little as 10 s |
| Radio operator | Artillery Targeting Accuracy | Shell scatter shrinks from 57 m to as little as 45 m |
| Radio operator | Radio Communication | In Arcade, enemies seen by any ally (aircraft included) appear on your minimap within 500 m by default. In Realistic, it extends the range at which ally-spotted enemies are shown by red arrows at the edge of your screen. Either range can grow to 1,000 m. |

## Cost to max each skill

Skills go up in half-level steps, except Repair Rank, which has its five stages. The highest possible crew level is 75 for aircraft, 150 for tanks and 100 for ships. Each step costs more than the one before it. For example, a tank crew member's Keen Vision costs 20 crew XP for the first half-level and 410 for the last.
