Every tank starts out with an automatic gearbox, but you can switch to a manual one. Automatic handles the shifting for you. Manual lets you pick the gear yourself and control engine revs, which helps on steep climbs and when you want to creep around at a steady, controlled pace. It is one more thing to manage in the middle of a fight, but it can be worth it.

## Manual or automatic?

| Feature | Automatic | Manual |
|---|---|---|
| Ease of use | Simple to learn, nothing extra to think about in battle | Takes practice and attention |
| Speed control | Drives as fast as it can | You choose exactly how fast to go |
| Torque | No direct control | Low gears give more pulling power, e.g. for climbing hills |
| Cruise control | Yes | No |
| Acceleration | Often quicker | With practice, you may accelerate faster than the automatic |

## Automatic and cruise control

Automatic is the default and needs no new keys: forward to go forward, back to go back.

Its handy extra is **cruise control**, which holds your speed for you at set steps, so you can keep a steady speed or angle without holding a movement key. By default E steps cruise control forward and Q steps it back. The current setting is shown in the bottom-left of the HUD, between the GEAR and RPM readouts.

From slowest to fastest, the steps are:

1. **CC R:** reversing.
2. Off: not shown on the HUD.
3. **CC 1**
4. **CC 2**
5. **CC MAX:** as fast as the vehicle will go.

Some tanks have gun stabilisers that only work up to a certain speed. These often get an extra **CC BATTLE** step, which holds the highest speed at which the stabiliser stays active.

## Switching to manual

Go to **Menu → Controls → Ground vehicles → Movement → Transmission mode** and bind a key to it. That key toggles between automatic and manual. When manual is active, an [M] appears on the HUD after the gear indicator.

## Driving a manual

### The HUD

Watch the readouts in the bottom-left corner:

- **GEAR:** the gear you are in, such as N for neutral.
- **RPM:** engine revolutions per minute.
- **SPD:** your speed, in whatever unit you have chosen in the options.

### Gears

Exact gearboxes vary from vehicle to vehicle, but all have at least a neutral (N), two forward gears (1 and 2) and one reverse gear (R1). Some vehicles have more forward gears or more than one reverse, but there is only ever one neutral, which does not move the vehicle at all. On a tank with 7 forward and 2 reverse gears, the order from bottom to top is R2, R1, N, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

### Keys

Shifting works like paddle shifters on the "Ground vehicle transmission gear / cruise control" bindings: E shifts up and Q shifts down by default on PC. Starting in N, two presses of E put you in 2nd; four presses of Q from there take you down to R2.

The other two movement keys change role in manual:

- **W (Ground Vehicle Throttle Up)** becomes the accelerator and drives you in the direction of the current gear: forward in a forward gear, backward in a reverse gear.
- **S (Ground Vehicle Throttle Down)** becomes the brake and stops the vehicle as quickly as it can. Brakes are not equally good on every vehicle.

### Getting moving

1. Shift into 1st and hold the accelerator.
2. When the RPM climbs and then levels off, shift up to 2nd. The revs dip a little and then recover.
3. Keep repeating this until you reach your top gear.
4. To stop, hold the brake until the tank comes to a halt.
5. To reverse, select R1 and hold the accelerator, shifting to R2 if your vehicle has one.

## When manual pays off

- **Steep hills.** If your tank cannot get up a slope in automatic, switch to manual and drop into 1st. You will go slower but have more torque and the best chance of making it.
- **Going downhill.** To avoid picking up too much speed and possibly damaging the vehicle, use a low gear such as 1st or 2nd on the way down.
- **Peeking from cover.** Creep out in 1st (or R1 if you are backing out) for fine control. While you line up the shot, shift into R1 (or 1st if you came out backwards) so you are already in gear to retreat. Fire, accelerate back into cover, and repeat when you have reloaded.

## Tips

- Practise in a test drive before taking manual into a real battle.
- Bind the transmission toggle to a key that is easy to reach, so you can flip to manual for a hill or a peek and back to automatic for everything else.
