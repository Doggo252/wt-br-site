A replay is often the clearest way to show a bug: instead of describing what happened, you let someone watch it. War Thunder keeps two kinds of replay, and they work quite differently.

## Client vs server replays

| | Client replay | Server replay |
|---|---|---|
| When it's made | When you leave a match or a custom battle | For every match played |
| Recorded by | Your own game | The server |
| Point of view | What your client knew during the match | The server's record of the match |
| Where you get it | A file on your computer | Through the website |
| What goes in a bug report | The replay file | A link to the match |

Because a client replay only holds what your game had access to during the match, it can be missing some details of the battle. A server replay is recorded on the server instead, so it isn't limited to what your client saw.

Two rules apply to both:

- **Replays expire with major patches.** Once a major update is released, the new game version can no longer play older replays, client or server.
- **Always add a timestamp.** Say where in the replay the problem happens, so nobody has to watch the whole match to find it.

## Finding a client replay

1. In the hangar, open **Community → Replays** from the top left corner.
2. A list of your replays opens, with the most recent at the top. If it helps, rename the one you want.
3. Click the folder button in the bottom left corner of the window to open the replay folder in your file explorer.
4. Upload the replay file from the match with the problem to your report, along with the timestamp.

## Finding a server replay in the game

1. Open the messages tab in the bottom right corner of the hangar.
2. Click the match where the problem happened to expand its details.
3. Click "View Server Replay". A browser window opens with the replay.
4. Click "copy link" and paste that link into your report, with a timestamp.

## Finding a server replay on the website

1. Go to warthunder.com/en/tournament/replay/ and make sure you're logged in.
2. Tick "Find replays with my participation".
3. Search for the mission name and click the right match.
4. Click "copy link" and paste it into your report, with a timestamp.
