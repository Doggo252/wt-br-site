The Gaijin Market is where players trade in-game items with each other. Everything is priced in Gaijin Coins (GJN), which you either buy with real money or earn by selling things on the Market. Gaijin doesn't set the prices: buyers and sellers do, so supply and demand decide what an item costs, and popular items can sell out. For current prices of tradable vehicles, see our [market tracker](/market/).

## Before you can trade

Trades between players can't be reversed. If someone stole your account, they could sell off your items and nobody could undo it. For that reason the Market only works once you have:

- linked your account to two-factor authentication, and
- confirmed your email address.

## Getting there

From the hangar, go to **Store → Market**, or open trade.gaijin.net in your browser. You log in with the same email and password you use for War Thunder. Always check the address of the login page before typing anything: it should be exactly `https://login.gaijin.net/en/`.

## What can be traded

- Vehicle coupons
- Camouflage coupons
- Decal coupons
- Profile picture coupons
- Trophy boxes
- Materials from crafting events

Keys for trophy boxes are also on the Market, but those come from Gaijin, not from other players.

## Finding your way around

The Market has three main sections:

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Inventory | Everything you own that can be put up for sale. Click an item to open its Market page. |
| Offers | Your active sell listings, your buy offers, and "Offer history", a record of every purchase and sale you've made. |
| Market | All items players are selling, with sorting and filters on the right to narrow the list. |

### An item's page

Each item has its own page with the details you need before trading:

- **Offers to buy:** what other players are willing to pay right now.
- **Offers to sell:** what sellers are asking for an instant sale.
- **Buy/Sell chart:** demand against supply. Green means the item is available; blue means there is a shortage.
- **Price history:** the average selling price day by day; hover over the line to see how many units sold.

If a seller lists an item at or below the highest buy offer, the sale goes through immediately.

## Selling an item

1. In the Market's Inventory, find the item. Tradable items carry a "G" icon.
2. Click "Buy/Sell" to open its page, then "Sell".
3. Set your price, accept the User Agreement and press "Sell" again.

If someone already has a buy offer at your price or higher, the item sells on the spot. Otherwise it is listed, and you can watch or change it under **Offers → Offers to sell**. Once it sells, it shows up in Offer history.

## Buying an item

You can buy at the lowest asking price on the item's page. If that price is too high for you, place your own buy offer from the same page instead; it stays in the Offers section and fills automatically if a seller comes down to your price.

## Using what you bought

Market items appear in the game in your inventory under **Store → Inventory**. Select one and press "Use" to apply it to your account. Think before you do: using an item can't be undone. A camouflage becomes tied to its vehicle for good, and a coupon turns into the vehicle, never the other way round. Many items have a "View" button so you can preview them first.

You also can't use a second copy of a unique item, such as a premium vehicle or a camouflage you already have; the game will tell you if you try. The only thing you can do with a duplicate is sell it.

## Buying Gaijin Coins

1. In the Gaijin Store, click the Gaijin Coin icon in the top bar to open your balance page.
2. Choose how many GJN you want and how to pay.
3. When the payment completes, the coins go into your Market wallet.

There are some restrictions:

- GJN can't be bought with a Steam wallet.
- GJN can't be bought for accounts connected to DMM.
- The Market isn't available at all on PS4, Xbox One, or DMM-connected accounts.

### Spending GJN outside the Market

GJN aren't locked to the Market. You can also spend them in the Gaijin Store, for example on Golden Eagles, by choosing "Pay from GJN balance" at checkout.
