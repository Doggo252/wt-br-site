If you build content for War Thunder yourself and Gaijin puts it in the game, you can get paid for it. The Revenue Share program covers user-generated content (UGC) made with the War Thunder CDK: 3D models of vehicles, cockpit models, camouflages and other kinds of content. The condition is simple: you have to be the author, and the content has to actually be used in War Thunder.

## How the payment is worked out

Gaijin chooses the payment method when it decides to use a piece of content:

| Content | Payment |
|---|---|
| 3D model of an aircraft, tank or ship | Fixed fee of $6,000 to $10,000 USD |
| 3D cockpit model | Fixed fee of $1,500 to $8,000 USD |
| Camouflage sold directly | Fixed fee of $120 to $800 USD |
| Camouflage sold inside a trophy box on the Gaijin Market | Share Revenue (see below) |
| Anything else | Another method, at Gaijin's discretion |

## Share Revenue

With Share Revenue you don't get a lump sum up front. Instead you receive **30%** of the income Gaijin makes from selling your item, either spread over a period of time or paid in one go.

When your work is sold as part of a bundle with other people's work, the 30% is divided in proportion to each author's part. Take a trophy box of 5 camouflages where you made one and the other four came from other authors or the developers. If that box brings in $1,500, your share is one fifth of the income at 30%, which comes to $90.

## Getting paid

Awards land in your Gaijin Coin (GJN) balance after each completed sale. You can spend them like any other GJN, or ask for the GJN earned through this program to be paid out in real money:

- in US dollars, at 1 GJN = 1 USD, or
- in euros, at the exchange rate on the day of payment.

A payout request is only accepted when all of the following are true:

- you have not already requested one this quarter (the limit is once per quarter)
- your balance of GJN earned through Revenue Share is above 500 GJN
- your account has two-factor authentication turned on
- the GJN you want to cash out were credited more than 3 months ago

Once a valid request is in, the money arrives within 90 calendar days. The full terms are set out in Gaijin's contribution agreement, which is worth reading before you submit anything.
