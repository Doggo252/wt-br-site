Warbonds (WB) are a side currency that you don't earn from battles directly. They are handed out as rewards on certain levels of the [Battle Pass](/guide/battle-pass/battle-pass/), and you spend them in the Warbond Shop. The shop gets a new stock list every time a Battle Pass season starts, so what is on offer depends on the season, on how far your Battle Pass has progressed, and on how many special tasks you have finished.

## What you can buy

The shop mixes progression helpers, vehicles and cosmetics:

- **Progression:** universal backups, boosters, orders and special tasks
- **Vehicles:** premium vehicles and vehicle test drives
- **Cosmetics and extras:** camouflages, decals, decorators, profile icons and talismans
- **Wagers**

### Items that come as a random trophy

Some goods are not sold directly. You buy a battle trophy instead, which works like a chest: it picks one item from a fixed list and you don't get to choose. It will never give you something you already own.

| Item type | What the trophy can contain |
|---|---|
| Orders, profile icons, wagers, decorators | One item from the trophy's own list |
| Camouflages, talismans | Only items for vehicles that are already in your account |

## Shop levels

The Warbond Shop has six levels of its own, and each one opens more of the stock list. The shop level simply follows your Battle Pass level, which you raise by completing daily and special tasks.

| Shop level | Battle Pass level needed |
|---|---|
| I | 1 |
| II | 11 |
| III | 23 |
| IV | 37 |
| V | 57 |
| VI | 80 |

Your current shop level is shown both in the Warbond Shop and in the Battle Tasks menu. The top left corner of the shop also shows how many days you have left to spend Warbonds on the current (or the previous) season's stock.

## Special tasks and medals

Special tasks are a kind of battle task that you buy in the Warbond Shop itself; the [Battle Pass](/guide/battle-pass/battle-pass/) page explains the permits you need for them. Each special task you finish awards a medal. Medals sit alongside shop levels as a second gate: some items stay locked until you have collected enough of them.

| Medals needed | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| 0 | Universal backups, boosters |
| 1 | Vehicle test drives, profile icons |
| 2 | Camouflages for Rank I–III aircraft and ground vehicles |
| 3 | Camouflages for Rank IV–V aircraft and ground vehicles, decals |
| 5 | Camouflages for Rank VI–VII aircraft and ground vehicles, decorators |
| 10 | Rank I–II premium vehicles |
| 12 | Talismans: Rank I–III aircraft and ground vehicles; Rank I–II bluewater ships and coastal vessels |
| 15 | Rank III premium vehicles (Rank II for coastal vessels) |
| 20 | Talismans: Rank IV aircraft and ground vehicles; Rank III bluewater ships and coastal vessels |
| 40 | Talismans: Rank V aircraft and ground vehicles; Rank IV bluewater ships |

## Your balance and the 3,600 cap

You can see how many Warbonds you hold at any time in the Warbond Shop or the Battle Tasks menu. They never expire and they keep piling up from season to season, but only up to a limit: an account can hold at most **3,600 Warbonds**.

If a Battle Pass reward would push you past 3,600, the game asks what you want to do:

- take the reward now and lose whatever goes over the cap, or
- leave the reward unclaimed until you have spent enough that the whole amount fits.

## When a new season begins

Your Warbonds carry over untouched. Two things go back to zero: your shop level and your special-task medals for the new season. The previous season's shop doesn't vanish straight away; it stays open for another 7 days, so you have a short window to spend on its stock.
