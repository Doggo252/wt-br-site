The Battle Pass is a seasonal reward track. Over each season you collect progress points, and every 10 points opens the next level of the track. Each level holds a prize: Silver Lions, boosters, backups, [Warbonds](/guide/economy/warbonds/), cosmetics and, at a few key levels, premium vehicles you can't get anywhere else at the time. Part of the track is free for everyone; the rest needs the paid pass.

You can take part once you have bought any researchable rank II vehicle in any nation. The Battle Pass window opens from the promo block on the right side of the hangar.

## Free track and paid pass

Every level is either marked "Free", meaning anyone can claim it, or reserved for pass holders. The free side is generous: in every season it includes one of the exclusive premium vehicles, and every Warbond reward in the main prize table (the first 75 levels) is free.

The pass itself is bought with Golden Eagles, and it doesn't matter when. Buying it late in a season doesn't cost you anything already earned: the moment you buy it, you can collect the paid rewards from every level you've already opened. How fast you gain progress points doesn't depend on owning the pass, either. The only reason not to wait is that you get the paid vehicles later.

### Improved Battle Pass

The Improved Battle Pass is the normal pass plus 15 levels opened on the spot, with their prizes ready to claim. Those 15 levels are 20% of the main prize table. It doesn't include any extra prizes beyond that.

### Buying levels

Once you own either version of the pass, you can also buy individual levels for Golden Eagles. Each level gets more expensive the more you've already bought (levels from the Improved pass count towards this):

| Levels already bought | Cost per level |
|---|---|
| Up to 15 | 75 Golden Eagles |
| 16–25 | 125 Golden Eagles |
| 26–35 | 175 Golden Eagles |
| 36 and more | 250 Golden Eagles |

The Improved pass is usually the cheaper way to get levels: its 15 levels come to 500 Golden Eagles, against 1,125 for 15 single levels at 75 each.

There's also a cap early on. In the first four weeks of a season you can buy at most 15 levels (no more than 150 progress points), whether through the Improved pass or one by one. From day 29 of the season the remaining levels can be bought too.

## Earning progress points

Points come from three places.

### Logging in

You get points with your daily login reward, once per day. The more days you've logged in during the season, the more each login is worth:

| Days logged in this season | Points per login |
|---|---|
| 1–21 | 1 |
| 22–42 | 2 |
| 43–63 | 3 |
| 64–77 | 4 |
| 78–91 | 5 |

This counter is separate from the general daily-login streak and doesn't reset if you skip days.

### Battle tasks

| Task | Points |
|---|---|
| Easy | 2 |
| Medium | 3 |
| Special | 5, plus a "Battle trophy" |

Special tasks have to be bought in the Warbond Shop, and you need a special permit to do that. You get a permit each time you complete a medium task. Permits stack up over the season so you can use them when it suits you, but you can only have one special task going at a time. At the end of the season, unused permits and unfinished tasks are wiped.

### Season challenges

Season challenges are the hard tasks of the season, done in battles in the main game modes. Each is worth 30 points, and finishing all 14 adds a bonus of 45 points. They appear on their own "Tasks" tab in the Battle Pass window and arrive gradually:

- 1 is available when the season starts,
- 1 more is added each week,
- 3 come as prizes on levels 21, 40 and 60.

From Season IV onwards, each weekly challenge has its own three-week window (in Season XXV, for example, the first runs from 21.10 to 11.11), while the three challenges from levels 21, 40 and 60 stay open until the season ends. Seasons II and III had 16 challenges instead of 14.

Daily tasks and challenges are tied to random battles and have restrictions on low-rank vehicles or battle rating, but you can earn points in every vehicle type: aircraft, ground vehicles, ships and helicopters.

## How a season's track is laid out

Each season lasts at least 13 weeks, and its length can be adjusted around other events. The levels never run out: after the last exclusive prize you can keep levelling and collecting rewards. In recent seasons the track has followed a steady pattern:

| Level | Reward |
|---|---|
| 9 | Test drive of the free vehicle (free) |
| 14 | 10 days of Premium account (a vehicle in Seasons I–VI) |
| 38 | An extra vehicle; from Season XVI a trophy with a random vehicle, mostly from earlier passes |
| 51 | The free premium vehicle as a non-tradable coupon (level 50 in Seasons I–IV), with its camouflage at 54 |
| 75 | The season's headline vehicle as a non-tradable coupon, with its camouflage at 79 |
| 100 | A loading screen (free) |
| 105 and 125 | Coupon upgrades for the two coupon vehicles (from Season III) |
| 150 | A title (free) |
| 76–99 | 80 WB per level, except 79 (camouflage) and 80 (850 WB and Warbond Shop level VI); 76, 85, 90 and 95 are free |
| 101–149 | 100 WB per level, apart from the levels above; 110, 120, 130 and 140 are free |
| 151 onwards | 80 WB per level, free |

Warbond Shop levels I to VI unlock at Battle Pass levels 1, 11, 23, 37, 57 and 80.

## Coupons and trading

The vehicles at levels 51 and 75 come as non-tradable coupons. The coupon upgrade at level 105 or 125 turns the matching coupon into a tradable one. Alternatively, a set of two upgrades can be used to buy the next season's Battle Pass.

An upgraded coupon can't be sold straight away. It becomes available for sale on the [Gaijin Market](/guide/economy/gaijin-market/) only after a waiting period, which varies by season but is never less than 75 days after the upgrade.

## When the season ends

- You can no longer earn progress points until the next season starts.
- Permits for special tasks are reset, so they can't be stockpiled across seasons.
- Everything you claimed stays yours and can be used without restriction, including the exclusive prizes.
